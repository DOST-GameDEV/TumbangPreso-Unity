# Hero kit decisions and distinct loadout jobs

Status: planning in progress, 2026-09-10. The owner explicitly authorized replacing
boring abilities and making every existing loadout choice justify its place.
Read LORE.md, ABILITY_REWORK_PLAN.md and PHILIPPINE_ABILITY_DIRECTION.md with this.
This records design candidates, not claims that they are already implemented or
balanced. Preserve saved variant IDs; update displayed names/copy only when the
actual function changes. Keep two options per ordinary skill slot and one readable
ultimate per hero. Do not add another loadout system.

## Character and tactical identity

| Hero | Lore expressed as a decision in play | Ultimate direction |
|---|---|---|
| Sean | Commits to a brave line and makes a decisive opening; his risk is where he ends up | A readable gather, airborne commitment, grounded impact and burning aftermath. Heavy motion without rubbery 40% body scaling |
| Zack | Finds and executes precise angles; uses timing and movement to convert a difficult throw | A focused storm strike with branching connections and clear charged-throw aftermath. Precision carries its force |
| Dante | Holds difficult space patiently and makes others respect his footing | A braced ground split propagating forward; the body drives the fracture. Keep readable floor and a visible escape direction |
| Cheska | Studies routes and places exactly enough ice to change them | A controlled gathering of cold, readable floor wave and distinct restraint. Keep real floor alignment and early-break cleanup |
| Nemu | Anticipates openings through her familiar; playful/uncanny movement and inward forces | Kuro becomes a giant raging ghost, actually pulling rivals and loose slippers inward, then visibly returns. Retain his idle personality |
| Phaister | Performs a carefully timed sporting trick; a setup earns its reveal | Invocation, inscriptions, alignment, eclipse payoff, sustained pulses and orderly release. Fictional lunar rune grammar, no copied sacred writing |

These are extraordinary sporting talents. No lasting injury, ruined neighborhood,
war or villain lore. Philippine influence should be specific and fitting, with
English copy, rather than pasted symbols or compulsory traditional costumes.

## Acceptance before closing a kit

For every default and alternate, capture one useful attacker situation and one
useful defender situation, or explicitly justify a role-specific trade. Compare
what it gives up as well as what it gains. A Gain+Cost arithmetic sum of zero does
not prove balanced play; retain the existing no-strict-upgrade intent while using
actual outcomes to decide whether an alternative earns its slot.

Verify actual contact, range, interruption, early end, repeated use and recovery.
Make body/FPP gestures, cast sound, world effect and descriptions match that exact
behavior. Keep visual danger honest during overlap and on kerbs/raised floors.
Check host, owner and observer paths as part of each rework; latejoin effects for
the other five kits remain an explicit network queue, not a solved general feature.

Phaister's current ultimate radius is 10.5 m and its constructor has no windup.
A central cast reaches all corners of the confined defender box. A grand ritual
needs an actual warning before control begins, not elaborate drawing after the
first curse. Measure escape opportunity and preserve the imposing event through
phase, motion and sound rather than indiscriminate area or brightness.
