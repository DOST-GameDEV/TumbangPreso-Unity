# Bring It Home: accepted production direction

Owner authorization: 2026-10-27, this chat. Build the new cinematic opening first,
review and iterate, then capture gameplay and finish the silent two-minute trailer.
The owner permits existing animation clips and newly authored animation. Work may
continue overnight. No paid generation/service is authorized.

The opening follows Rago's throw at an ordinary can, reveals the stadium AFTER
contact, then establishes Dante restoring the can and controlling the route to
Rago's loose slipper. Rago wants it back. The closing cinematic resolves that
retrieval and echoes it in a newly authored neighborhood scene.

## Hard boundaries

- 0:00-0:20: original trailer cinematic opening.
- 0:20-1:28: captured native gameplay only.
- 1:28-1:52: original trailer cinematic ending.
- 1:52-2:00: actual TUMP identity and accurate closing copy.

Gameplay is forbidden in the cinematic slots. Existing map films, ultimate films
and game cutscenes cannot substitute for the new cinematic scenes. Reusing models,
sets and animation clips as materials is permitted. Direct new performances,
blocking, lighting and camera work. Preserve TUMP's cute blocky cast and flat faces.
No gameplay markers, aim lines, nameplates, red taya rings, hit text or badges.
One consistent 16:9 picture. No archive/VHS looks or historical dates.

The team creates ALL sound and music. Deliver silent picture and frame-accurate
sync notes after picture timing settles. Do not add temporary music or SFX.
Human voices only if the team later supplies them. All authored copy is English.

## Quality bar

Readable can/slipper, planted weight, prop contact, anticipation and follow-through,
intent through the body, no sliding or camera clipping. Establish the can, loose
slipper, defender, attacker and exit. Cause precedes effect. Review sequences and
the complete cut; contact sheets alone do not certify motion quality.
