# TUMP trailer plan: "PANDAIGDIG"

Owner brief, 2026-10-27: a two-minute trailer that is **cinematic, nostalgic and fun**, mixes
new in-engine cinematics with real gameplay, gets hype, and is set in a world where the
Philippines made it and tumbang preso is the football of the planet, with a world tournament
every year.

This fits the existing [LORE](../../LORE.md) premise ("tumbang preso grew into one of the
world's favorite spectator sports. The Philippines remains its cultural home"). The trailer
shows that world; it does not promise a tournament mode the game does not have.

---

## 1. The pitch in one breath

> A kid throws a tsinelas on a Tondo street at sunset. It lands in a sold-out Philippine Arena.
> In this world, the biggest sport on Earth started on our streets, and every year the whole
> country stops to watch the Pandaigdig final. **It began on an ordinary street.**

Three feelings, and where each one lives:

| Feeling | Where it shows up |
|---|---|
| **Nostalgic** | Golden-hour street, 4:3 home-video look, chalk courts, sari-sari TV, lola on a monobloc, the kid we follow |
| **Cinematic** | Widescreen 2.39 stadium shots, tunnel walk-out, slow push-ins, the held wind-up before the last throw |
| **Fun** | Laughing at the taya, chickens scattering, near misses, hero powers, the "Isa pa?" ending |

## 2. What the research says (and how we use it)

Sources: Derek Lieu's trailer editing guides (structure, cold opens, stopdowns, music structure,
"show the dream, not the job", avoiding generic trailers), GMTK and GDC talks on memorable game
trailers, and the reference trailers in section 9.

| # | Rule | How TUMP's trailer applies it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook in 5 to 10 seconds.** People scroll. | Street throw match-cuts into a roaring stadium by 0:06. The whole idea lands before 0:12 |
| 2 | **Genre, Hook, Content**, in that order. | 0:00 to 0:12 tells you it is a sport and a world. Then the gameplay. Then the heroes |
| 3 | **No unknown logos first.** | Studio and logo only at the end slate |
| 4 | **Show the dream, not the job.** | The dream is "becoming a legend of the street game", not "press button to throw" |
| 5 | **Excite and educate.** Tell, show, repeat. | Short title cards, each followed by the exact gameplay it describes (Act 3) |
| 6 | **Music is the skeleton.** Slow open, build, bigger build, epic, fake-out silence, title. | Section 6 maps one music cue to these exact sections |
| 7 | **Stopdowns** release tension and say "something big is coming". | Two of them: before Hero Strike (1:00) and before the final throw (1:47) |
| 8 | **Keep the sound effects on.** | The tin crack, rubber slap and crowd are the trailer's punctuation |
| 9 | **Clear, readable shots.** One idea per shot, good eye trace. | Same bar as our [map films](../MAP_FILM_METHOD.md): emotion readable from the wide shot, nobody blocking the subject |
| 10 | **Be specific, not generic.** Lead with what only you have. | Nobody else has tsinelas, a lata, a taya and a Philippines-as-superpower world. Lead with those |

## 3. The world (trailer lore bible)

Everything below is fiction for the trailer, consistent with LORE.md. Pick names in section 11.

**The tournament: PANDAIGDIG** ("worldwide"). The yearly world championship, like football's
World Cup or esports' Worlds. Each year has its own host city, poster and anthem.
Locally there is also **PAMBANSA** ("national"), the national championship you qualify through.
"Pambansa to Pandaigdig" is the career path every kid knows.

**The trophy: ANG GINTONG LATA** (The Golden Can). A dented can cast in gold, on a stone base
shaped like a chalk court. Winners do not polish the dent out.

**It is an individual sport** (LORE: rankings recognise individual players, there are no
national teams). Flags beside names on broadcast graphics, crowds from every country, but four
athletes on the court. Like tennis or boxing, not football clubs.

**What a successful Philippines looks like, in small details:**

| Detail | Shot it becomes |
|---|---|
| Signature tsinelas are the new sneakers. Drops sell out | Tsinelas hung for sale at a sari-sari like jerseys; a giant tsinelas billboard over the city |
| Heroes are on public transport | LRT/MRT train wrapped in hero art passing over Ermita; a jeepney hand-painted with "RAGO" |
| When the final is on, the whole country stops (the Pacquiao-fight memory every Filipino has) | Empty streets, TVs flickering in every window, a barangay crowded round one TV |
| Every street has a court | Chalk lines on asphalt everywhere; a kid redrawing them after rain |
| Rituals | The taya taps the can three times before round one; winners sign their tsinelas and throw them into the crowd |
| History | Faux archive: "PANDAIGDIG 1974, MAYNILA" newsreel; 1990s VHS of kids copying a famous throw |
| The world comes to us | Flags of many nations in the Arena; tourists learning to shout "TAYA!" |

**Hard rules:** no real people or real officials in the faux archive; no invented history
attached to real monuments; no guns or injury (LORE); no real brands. A fictional sponsor
("Kanto Sardinas") is fine.

### 7a. Cinematic approach: League / Valorant style, in TUMP's look

Owner, 2026-10-27: we make our own animations and cutscenes, the way League of Legends and
Valorant make their cinematics. So the [NEW] shots are **real cinematic scenes**, not gameplay
with a moved camera:

| What Riot does | What we do |
|---|---|
| Purpose-built cinematic sets, not the game map | Dressed sets that can leave the court: Mayon's slope, a Vigan cobbled street, the Arena tunnel, a sari-sari interior |
| Hand-keyed character performance, every gesture acted | Custom keyframed acting per shot (the pose-key system in `tools/author_ultimate_intros.py` and MapFilm's acting layer), pushed past gameplay animation |
| Film camera: lenses, rack focus, slow push-ins, handheld energy | Authored camera paths, shallow depth of field, speed ramps |
| Lighting as emotion (Valorant "Duality", League "Warriors") | Golden hour for nostalgia, cold floodlight for the final, warm TV glow for the barangay |
| Characters stay recognisable | TUMP's cute blocky cast, flat faces, emotion in the body ([art direction](../Art_Direction.md)). Cinematic, never realistic |

**Bonus beat this unlocks (Act 4):** each hero card opens with a **one-second origin flash**
before the gameplay: young Rago on Mayon's slope throwing at a can; Dante marking the plaza court
at dawn; Nemu on Rizal Boulevard with Kuro; Phaister on Capul under the moon; Amihan throwing with
the wind through Vigan; Paete's roots under Mount Makiling. That is the League "who are these
people" feeling inside four seconds per hero.

**Stretch option:** the same scenes can become a separate 60 to 90 second story cinematic
("Isa Pa", the kid who never stops asking for one more game, growing up into a Pandaigdig
finalist) released before the trailer, the way Riot releases a cinematic and a gameplay trailer
separately.

**Existing assets reused:** ArenaIntro (bowl crane, screen shuffle), the six ultimate
cutscenes, the Tondo map film's laughing beat, ambient life (chickens, pets, train), arena crowd
audio, ability VFX and SFX.

**Gameplay capture ([GAME])** uses the replay viewer: free camera, hide UI, 0.25x slow motion,
exact-time entry (see [LOCAL_REPLAYS](../LOCAL_REPLAYS.md)). Plan: play a few real matches on
each map, save the replays, then direct the camera moves afterwards. First-person shots come
from a player's own screen.

## 10. Production order

1. **Owner picks** the decisions in section 11.
2. **Animatic (Claude):** a rough 2:00 cut with stills and title cards on the music skeleton,
   so timing is judged before anything is polished.
3. **Gameplay capture (team + Claude):** play and save replays; Claude logs the best moments.
4. **Build T1, T8, T4 first** (they carry the emotion), then T3, T7, T2, T9.
5. **Graphics T5, T6, T10.**
6. **Team records** the four voice lines; music is finalised.
7. **Edit, mix, colour**, then the 30 s and 15 s cuts.

Follows the project's film bars: emotion readable from the wide shot, no blocked subjects, light
as mood, every sound landing on a cut or action.
