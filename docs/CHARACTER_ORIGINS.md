# Character origins and court stories

Owner-requested direction, 2026-09-14. These are original fictional biographies
for TUMP's existing sporting world. Real places ground the stories; they do not
make these athletes historical people or representatives of everyone from a region.
The existing six names remain useful court names. No roster/save ID changes.
Rafi is a working name for the deferred seventh hero, subject to the owner's choice.

## Sean: San Fernando, Pampanga

Home court: **Eskinita**, a fictional neighbourhood lane in San Fernando.

Character-select line: **Waits for one opening. Makes it count.**

Long introduction:

Sean grew up playing between the houses of San Fernando, where an afternoon match
could hold up the walk home for hours. He helped assemble lantern frames after
school and learned to notice the small mistake that throws a whole shape off.
On court he is just as particular: one chosen angle, one committed run. Zack
calls him slow. Sean keeps the score. His fire gets the crowd's attention, but
the clean retrieval afterward is the part he wants them to remember.

His patience is a strength and a flaw: he can wait past a useful chance while
looking for a perfect one. Fire-light stories support the effect language;
lantern craft informs his personal concentration, not a costume or sacred power.

## Zack: Pasig, Metro Manila

Home court: **Sa Bubong**, the condo roofdeck where he grew up playing.

Character-select line: **Finds the angle before you see the opening.**

Long introduction:

Zack learned tumbang preso on the roofdeck of his family's condo in Pasig. The
court changed with laundry days, resident gatherings and whoever had claimed the
shade, so he got good at finding an angle before anyone else settled in. Now he
makes difficult plays look casual and casual plays unnecessarily difficult.
Sean is usually the first to call him on it. Zack wants a national title, partly
for himself and partly so the neighbours can say they used to beat him upstairs.

Pasig is the fictional condo's chosen locality, not a claim that the current map
copies a real building. Electricity belongs to Zack's talent, not a named deity.

## Dante: Montalban, Rizal

Home court: **Bayan Plaza**, a fictional civic court in his home municipality.

Character-select line: **Holds the difficult space. Refuses to be rushed.**

Long introduction:

In Montalban, Dante was the player trusted to keep a crowded game fair: settle
the argument, straighten the can, give the youngest player another try. He still
arrives early to mark out the plaza court. That patience becomes stubbornness
when a rival tries to move him. His stone armour and shaking faults make the
mountain stories he grew up hearing feel close enough to touch. Cheska admires
his discipline. She also knows exactly how long he will defend a bad position.

Bernardo Carpio's mountain struggle informs force and divided stone. The demonic
horns and molten seams are Dante's fictional visual expression; the folk hero
is not described as a demon. No real monument or church acquires invented history.

## Cheska: La Trinidad, Benguet

Home court: a neighbourhood court in **La Trinidad**, currently outside the
playable map set. Do not mislabel Bayan or an urban map as her hometown.

Character-select line: **Reads the space. Leaves you the harder route.**

Long introduction:

Cheska grew up in La Trinidad and prefers the court before it gets noisy. She
walks its edges, tests the footing and remembers where everyone likes to run.
Friends mistake her quietness for caution until their easiest route freezes
shut. Away from competition she is the practical one who remembered the water
and checked the ride home. Losing bothers her more than she admits, especially
when an untidy play works. Dante can make her wait; Zack can make her improvise.
She is learning to enjoy both problems.

Benguet's highland terrain and cool climate anchor her atmosphere. Her ice is
fictional magic, not local permanent snow. Residence alone does not establish
Ibaloi, Kankanaey, Kalanguya or another ethnic identity for her.

## Nemu: Dumaguete, Negros Oriental

Home court: a small **Dumaguete** neighbourhood court, currently off-screen.

Character-select line: **Looks distracted. Already knows your next move.**

Long introduction:

Nemu grew up in Dumaguete, taking the long way home along the waterfront and
stopping whenever a game had one place left. She seems to miss half the
conversation, then remembers the detail everyone else forgot. Kuro has always
been part of that routine: a curious little shadow at her shoulder, an alarming
presence when a match turns serious. She refuses to explain him the same way
twice. Phaister keeps trying to catch her by surprise. Nemu quietly saves those
attempts for the walk home.

Kuro is a personal fictional companion, not a claim about local ancestor spirits.
Keep the restored expressive small ghost and current monster transformation.

## Phaister: Capul, Northern Samar

Origin: **Capul, Northern Samar**. Current practice court: **Ilalim ng Tulay**,
near the project's existing Gilmore, Quezon City reference.

Character-select line: **Sets the stage. Lets you discover the trick.**

Long introduction:

Phaister grew up on Capul listening to stories about a moon that could disappear
into a serpent's mouth. When she moved to Quezon City, she found a regular game
beneath the rail line and an audience that was difficult to impress. That only
made her more ambitious. She treats a match like a carefully timed reveal:
invite a chase, leave a false opening, close the circle. The trouble is that she
occasionally enjoys the setup more than winning. Nemu notices. Phaister wishes
she would at least look surprised.

Her fictional lunar writing is not Inabaknon script or a documented ritual.
Birthplace and present-day court need not be identical. Do not move Ilalim out
of its established Gilmore setting to force a one-map-one-birthplace rule.

## How this reaches the game

Character selection should show origin and the short personality line beside
the actual retained model. Keep skill descriptions accessible as mechanics.
The long introduction belongs in a readable character story/reveal view, including
the requested unlock presentation. It is optional reading, never a forced delay.

Source audit currently finds all six heroes available; existing unlocks concern
ability variants, cosmetics and rewards. Do not invent paid locks or silently
remove existing access to manufacture a character-unlock screen. Share biography
data with the introduction view and future genuine hero-unlock event. UI wiring
and aspect/focus verification remain explicit tasks, not claims made by this file.

The four existing map identities remain intact: Sean/Eskinita, Zack/Sa Bubong,
Dante/Bayan, Phaister's adopted court/Ilalim. Cheska and Nemu have off-screen home
courts until a separately scheduled map expansion. All athletes can play all maps.

## Research and creative boundaries

- [Benguet provincial profile](https://benguet.gov.ph/about-the-province/):
  mountainous terrain, cool climate and distinct regional communities. Supports
  Cheska's setting, not a claim of ice magic or assigned ethnicity.
- [National Museum: Abaknon worldview](https://www.nationalmuseum.gov.ph/2021/10/29/abaknon-and-their-view-of-the-world/):
  Capul, lunar knowledge and moon-swallowing serpent account. Supports Phaister's
  remembered story, not her invented spell writing or a national universal belief.
- [CCP: Bernardo Carpio](https://epa.culturalcenter.gov.ph/3/82/2199/):
  mountain struggle in the Montalban setting and earthquake association. Modern
  artwork on that page is not source art for the game.
- [PIA: San Fernando lantern tradition](https://pia.gov.ph/features/in-phs-christmas-capital-giant-lanterns-illuminate-century-old-tradition/):
  local craft context for Sean. His family, after-school work and sporting life
  are invented, not quoted from an artisan's biography.
- [Dumaguete City: Rizal Boulevard](https://dumaguetecity.gov.ph/2018/11/19/paseo-de-rizal-or-the-rizal-boulevard/):
  waterfront setting for Nemu. No exact school, household or historic incident
  has been attributed to her.
