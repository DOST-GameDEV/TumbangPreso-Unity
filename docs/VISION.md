# Vision

Four players, one rotating taya, a lata and slippers. **The tension is the
retrieval, not the throw.** Keep the run back into danger, the chase and the escape
readable. The [historical vision](archive/cleanup-2026-10-25/docs/VISION.md)
preserves the team's original reasoning and old measurements.

## Two first-class modes

Classic is the street game without hero powers. Hero Strike adds distinct kits,
timing and counterplay. Both normally play eight rounds: each seat defends twice.
Custom lengths are configurable. Neither mode is permission to neglect the other.
Street Hype was removed; do not restore its mechanic or meter.

## Readability

- A fight must still show the lata, chalk, players and retrieval routes.
- Skills need a readable anticipation, commitment, travel/active beat, contact,
  victim response and recovery. Body, first person, world effects and sound agree.
- Normal skills use restrained footprints; an ultimate may dominate a moment.
  Measure the current implementation instead of copying historical area tables.
- Spend visual complexity on purposeful shape and motion. Stacked floor discs,
  whiteout and repeated labels do not make an effect more understandable.
- Icons communicate an ability's job. Character select teaches; held help recalls;
  the live HUD shows action/readiness, without explanatory sentences.
- Prompts use live bindings. Spectators need understandable controls and a stable
  view of the same accepted actions, skills, props and cutscenes as participants.

## Competitive rules

- The host decides scored outcomes. `MatchDirector.AddScore` owns every point.
- Rules stay in engine-free Core; contact uses the existing measured geometry.
- Defender is derived from the round, not accumulated. Bots use the same intents.
- Do not reward waiting. Pause/ultimate holds stop simulation effects and clocks.
- Preserve stable identity separately from permission to control a seat.

## Authored identity

Keep the cute blocky world, flat faces, individual characters, supplied UI art
and the Filipino street-game context. Better motion means convincing weight,
contact and timing inside that style. Preserve authored kits and appearance.
Catalogs and unlock gates live in source, not duplicated counts in this document.
