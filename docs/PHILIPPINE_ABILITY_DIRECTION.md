## What the owner asked for

Make the abilities, their descriptions and their presentation feel more Filipino
across **all six heroes**, drawing on actual folklore or other fitting cultural
references. Keep English player-facing copy; avoid Tagalog unless a particular
proper term is genuinely needed. Embed culture naturally, without decorative
nationalism, token symbols or forced jokes. Complete the whole rework, not only
this plan. Preserve Grand Coven's phased grand spell and Nemu's cute-to-giant,
raging, inward-pulling ghost transformation.

Character models/outfits stay kept. Skill/ghost/summon models, animation, sound,
effects and existing functions remain in scope. The later map stage includes the
rejected trees, installed-asset inventory and a thorough plan for all three maps.
Do not let the cultural direction revive the stopped character-redesign queue.

## Working approach

The heroes remain fictional athletes in TUMP's sporting world. They are not being
renamed after deities or presented as literal reconstructions of rituals. Give
each kit one coherent source of inspiration and carry it through the action,
materials, motion and sound. A foreign player should understand the skill; a
Filipino player should recognize something familiar without a glossary.

Keep the existing hero names and initially keep the existing English ability
names, especially the requested Grand Coven. Saved IDs never change. Cultural
specificity should work even when the player never opens a description. A short
flavor phrase may support clear tactical copy, but a folklore lecture should not
replace the explanation of what a button does.

The sources below support particular motifs, not every creative detail in the
proposals. The game-specific designs and mechanics below are original adaptations.
Regional traditions are not interchangeable and are not all Tagalog. Avoid claims
that one account is the single belief of every Filipino community.

## Reference base and limits

1. [National Museum: Abaknon worldview](https://www.nationalmuseum.gov.ph/2021/10/29/abaknon-and-their-view-of-the-world/).
   The museum documents Capul/Abaknon lunar knowledge and an eclipse explained as a
   snake swallowing the moon. This supports a moon-devouring/eclipse motif for
   Phaister. It does not document her fictional magic circle, runes or spell phases.
2. [CCP Encyclopedia: Bernardo Carpio in Rodel Tapaya's work](https://epa.culturalcenter.gov.ph/3/82/2199/)
   and [NHCP: Pamitinan Cave](https://philhistoricsites.nhcp.gov.ph/registry_database/pamitinan-cave/).
   These connect the giant/strongman legend to Montalban's mountain setting and
   struggles associated with earthquakes. Borrow bracing mass and divided stone,
   not the modern artwork's images or political allegory.
3. [NCCA: Talisman, Leadership, and Power](https://ncca.gov.ph/about-culture-and-arts/in-focus/talisman-leadership-and-power-the-belief-in-magical-potency-continues-to-pervade-filipino-life/).
   The indexed article discusses protective amulets and their place in several
   Philippine societies. Its direct page returned 403 during research; the
   indexed text was available. Use the broad protective-charm idea, not copied
   religious inscriptions, claims of efficacy or a fabricated universal ritual.
4. [USC Digital Folklore Archives: Aswangs](https://folklore.usc.edu/aswangs/).
   A collected family/diaspora account describes frightening transformations and
   varying shapeshifting beings. This is one documented account, not a universal
   taxonomy. Nemu borrows the dramatic contrast of familiar appearance and hidden
   predatory form; she is not being relabeled as a literal aswang species.
5. [The Aswang Project: Santelmo](https://www.aswangproject.com/santelmo/).
   This secondary source quotes Ramos and discusses distinct regional fire-light
   accounts, including bouncing/moving fireballs. Use the wandering-light motif;
   do not impose one origin story or copy modern comic illustrations. Additional
   local research exists in [the Arteche narrative study](https://rsisinternational.org/journals/ijriss/view/wandering-flame-a-narrative-study-of-santelmo-in-arteche-eastern-samar),
   which is a localized study, not proof of a national canon.
6. [PAGASA: Climate of the Philippines](https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/information/climate-philippines).
   Tropical/maritime moisture, rainfall and altitude-related cooling support the
   weather/place direction. This is natural setting context, not folklore. Do not
   turn the kits into a disaster simulation or quote old climate averages as current weather.
7. [UPLB repository: Makiling biodiversity and its guardian legend](https://www.ukdr.uplb.edu.ph/etd-capstone/218/).
   This recent student research identifies the Makiling guardian story. Use only
   that limited context with the broader mountain/water setting; do not copy its
   game, art or invented transformation rules. There is no claim here that
   Philippine tradition has an ice goddess or that Cheska is Maria Makiling.

No source images, sacred music or modern copyrighted character designs have been
adopted as game assets by this plan. Use original geometry/audio and the project's
licensed library. References inform the work, rather than becoming pasted-on art.

## How each action changes

### Sean

- **Flame Rush:** flame travels as a compact, purposeful surge with a torn ember
  wake. Let a few fire-light shapes linger along the actual path, never as new
  autonomous creatures. Hip coil, push-off and braced arrest carry the force.
- **Ignition Cannon:** a small, restless ember gathers around the actual slipper
  and settles into a hot core before release. The empowered throw and impact must
  be reviewed, not only the activation. Correct the pink-looking path only after
  tracing the actual material/tint. Sound should kindle, tighten and then release.
- **Supernova:** draw the wandering flame into one controlled mass, rise, descend
  and land as the main event. Ash and heat settle into the grounded crater. The
  impact should have a strong short crack and low body, not sustained loud noise.

The source motif is wandering fire; the athlete's propulsion and impact are TUMP's
fiction. Do not add fireball homing or other new gameplay to make the reference literal.

### Zack

- **Bolt Sprint:** purposeful skating cadence, tiny contact sparks and a bounded
  electrical wake with the established delay. Use a short accelerating chatter,
  wheel/contact rhythm and a distinct relaxation at the end of the actual boost.
- **Magnet:** one clear return route with a dry pickup/return snap and a compact
  charged state. Street metal resonance can support the sound. Keep the current
  narrow trace and measured grip; no radial discharge toward unrelated objects.
- **Thunderstrike:** a sudden local storm event after a readable call: rising
  tension, a branched stroke that actually reaches the aimed point, a hard contact
  crack and a receding roll. Wind/rain-like textures are sound/presentation accents,
  not new weather mechanics. Preserve legibility below the viaduct and near wires.

No unverified storm-god attribution is needed. Avoid turning the lightning into
continuous colored lighting that erases the retained face and clothes.

### Dante

- **Seismic Stomp:** a planted support foot, a compact downward action and a fracture
  that visibly starts at contact. Use a few layered stone pieces with meaningful
  scale and a low dust front. The visual should communicate weight, not confetti.
- **Demonic Carapace:** interlocking stone ward plates close to the body, with a
  coherent protective silhouette. A measured lock/strain/release sound follows the
  actual immunity period. Keep the caster's hands and first-person view usable.
- **Titan Fissure:** brace as though resisting mountains, then release force into a
  deliberate forward split. Pillars should be carved, irregular masses rising
  from the fracture and grounded at their bases. The giant-struggle inspiration
  belongs in movement and the separation of stone, not a new giant summon.

Retain the existing earth/magma identity and gameplay contracts. The ward geometry
is original fiction; it is not an authentic amulet replica or copied prayer.

### Cheska

- **Permafrost Sheet:** thin, fractured ice over the actual street, formed by a
  precise sweeping gesture. A cool surface sheen, quieter center and frosted edge
  preserve sight of slippers. A brief spreading formation replaces the platform,
  five decorative spikes and central upright cutout.
- **Ice Barricade:** clear water-like mass locks into three fractured uprights.
  Their bases, occupied volume and growth should agree. Use cold interior depth
  and frosted edges, with concise forming and breaking sounds.
- **Glacial Nova:** controlled inward gathering followed by an abrupt outward
  fracture. Low mist and sparse crystal fragments should locate the release.
  Victim encasement must look like ice around a readable person rather than a
  cyan box. The motion ends in poised stillness after the forceful release.

The local anchor is upland moisture, springs and territorial calm, not a false
historical claim about ice magic. Use local material/water sound references where
licensed; avoid putting literal food items or regional costume tokens in the spell.

### Nemu

- **Phantom Veil:** a familiar silhouette slips into a spectral phase, with a short
  afterimage/rim and inward air. Fix the held-slipper transition and ineffective
  sustained-speed path after reproducing the intended behavior. Avoid purple floodlight.
- **Astral Hijack:** the ghost separates with an unmistakable visual link, then
  trades places in a decisive departure/arrival pair. Give the projection a real,
  recognizable model and readable flight/lifetime instead of loose bloom effects.
- **Devouring Seance:** the cute ghost's expression and proportions change through
  anticipation into a giant raging form. Keep enough shared facial/silhouette
  cues for it to be the same ghost. Its mouth/body action and inward paths must
  explain the pull. Stage escalation, furious intake, sustaining rage and collapse
  back to the cute form. A deep intake/roar can be scary without gore or visual clutter.

The transformation draws on a documented dramatic pattern, not an exact monster
classification. Keep the ground boundary honest and the can, opponents and loose
slippers trackable during the giant form. Inspect the actual companion/hero link;
do not satisfy the requirement with a separate generic maw under an unchanged ghost.

### Phaister

Her whole kit shares an original lunar inscription grammar: crescents, binding
strokes and aligned divisions with consistent visual meaning. These are fictional
runes, not decorative gibberish labeled as a Philippine writing system.

- **Hex:** a small binding inscription written with a purposeful gesture. A few
  clear marks form in order; active and releasing states use different motion.
- **Shadow Blink:** a dark passage opens like a narrow break in the inscription,
  then closes at departure as the arrival mark resolves. Keep the two endpoints
  and sounds spatially distinct, with the true arrival footprint visible.
- **Grand Coven:** keep the explicit grand-magic requirement. Start with a readable
  outer warning and a sparse invocation. Primary circles and rune groups write
  themselves; selected groups align and lock. Crescent/serpentine divisions and
  the darkening moon evoke the eclipse reference. The payoff brings the eclipse/
  coven and the actual control event, followed by readable repeated pulses and
  ordered unbinding. Complexity must have a hierarchy and phases.

Do not use every ring at peak brightness or rotate every glyph continuously.
The phases must agree with the real warning/curse times. The existing 10.5 m reach,
missing constructor windup and confined-defender case require functional review,
as recorded in the main ability plan. Do not hide an already-active curse behind
an unfinished visual warning.
