# Kuro design references

These are generated design concepts, not Unity screenshots or shipped models.
They explored the owner's requested cute-to-raging purple spirit, imposing scale,
large maw, reaching hands and floating eyes. References to Hu Tao's ghost and
Gastly concern expressive spirit qualities, not copying their character designs.

The realistic giant-direction/single/mesh-input studies informed an idea the owner
liked, but realistic reconstruction and its voxel/faceted conversions were
explicitly rejected. Do not reuse those models as an accepted art direction.

clean-block-direction-concept-2026-09-10.png is the later shape-language reference.
The actual replacement is independently authored in Blender from clean large
forms by tools/author_clean_kuro_rage.py, with its rig and small form authored by
the related scripts. Judge the actual Unity captures linked from
../../reports/improvement-2026-09-10/kuro-matching-forms.md.
