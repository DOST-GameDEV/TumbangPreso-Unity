# World composition review

The later partial implementation is documented in
[map-checkpoint.md](map-checkpoint.md), with its continuation plan in
../../MAP_FINAL_PASS.md. It replaces the first shade-tree pass, refines prop
grouping/civic geometry/utilities and records actual test limits. The owner now
permits unfinished maps in the wrap-up handoff; this is not full map approval.

The initial matched views are in `Logs/improvement-baseline-v1`, from the
`4623348` runtime before the presentation changes. These are composite places,
not surveyed reproductions. Reference photographs are used for observation;
their pixels and advertisements do not ship in the game.

| Map | Reference observation | Large shapes and materials | Quiet space and landmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eskinita | [Project 8 sari-sari frontage, Exec8](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sari-sari_Store.JPG): corrugated canopy, shaded counter, grille, everyday goods, timber supports | Close houses with upper balcony depth, louvered ventilation and rainwater pipes; concrete and aged timber with galvanized eaves | Keep the court clear. A properly modeled counter gives the roadside shop purpose without invented slogans |
| Bayan Plaza | [Pila church and plaza, Ralff Nestor Nacor](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pila_Church_%26_Town_Plaza,_Laguna,_Jul_2026_(1).jpg): broad open center, shade at the edge, articulated church/belfry | A pitched pediment, recessed arched portal, masonry courses and an open belfry; warm stone with timber and a tiled civic roof | The open court remains the dominant ground shape. Church and tower carry identity, with restrained perimeter paving |
| Ilalim ng Tulay | [Gilmore Avenue and MRT2 right of way](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gilmore_Avenue_and_MRT2_RoW.jpg): heavy structural cap, guideway joints, weathered concrete, layered shop edges | Transverse caps, longitudinal underside ribs, bearing pads and drainage against the existing deck; neutral concrete and intact shop/vehicle materials | Preserve the clear chalk carriageway, PC Express/pisonet/pares edges and original jeepney livery. Shade must retain a readable slipper and escape route |

The team's TUMP.pdf pages 1-7 and 36-40 were rendered and reviewed. Their useful
world direction is bold silhouette, tactile material and warm printed color.
The new world geometry does not copy interface ornaments onto every facade.

The first modeled iteration deepened Eskinita's frontage and articulated the
guideway. Its runtime render exposed two things to revise: civic facade details
faced away from the court, and Ilalim's underside was too dark. Neither is accepted
on the strength of successful import. The next render must verify orientation,
shade detail and physical placement before this map batch is complete.
