# Airburst reference research, October 2

Scope: Amihan's ultimate (AIRBURST) presentation. The owner named VALORANT's Miks
ultimate as the primary visual reference because the two powers are close in
shape: a build-up, then a directed forward release that knocks players back.
Other references below test that reading against a small cute retrieval court.

## How this was gathered, and its limits

Footage was played muted in a local browser, paused and seeked; decoded frames
were drawn into timestamped contact sheets and inspected. Frame hashes were
checked so a stale frame was never described as a new one. Two sampling passes
returned identical hashes because the browser pane was hidden and the decoder
stopped producing frames; those passes are rejected and were redone with the
pane visible. Nothing was watched as continuous playback and **no audio was
heard**. This is still-frame evidence at the cited times, not millisecond
timing. Video frames are not committed here; the URLs and timestamps reproduce
every observation.

## What transfers to Airburst

1. **The charge lives in the hands.** Miks's build-up is on his palms, not in the
   air around him. Amihan's equivalent is the air she cups and packs at her hip.
2. **Outline first, then a filling meter.** The floor tells where at once and how
   soon by growing intensity. Airburst's live 1.5 s has the same job.
3. **A counter-move before release.** Hands pull back and apart, then drive
   together. Anticipation opposite the release.
4. **Thin fronts with clear interiors, staggered two or three deep.** The strike
   is drawn by its edges; the court stays visible through it.
5. **Fast recovery while the effect keeps travelling.** The caster is back in
   play before the wave is finished.
6. **(Gale)** The preview is exactly the gameplay shape, and a wind front made of
   separate pieces keeps the floor readable on a small court.

## Rejected

- Miks's orange and gold, speaker-cone palm discs, circular sound rings, screen
  warming, Deafen and the ring tunnel ride: his identity and fiction, not wind.
- The trailer's one-frame white burst: TUMP forbids full-screen white flashes.
- Riding the wave in the cutscene: Airburst's cutscene plays before its live
  dodge window, so it must not show the release at all.
- Venti's vortex and lifted crowd, Gale's snow: other characters' identities.
