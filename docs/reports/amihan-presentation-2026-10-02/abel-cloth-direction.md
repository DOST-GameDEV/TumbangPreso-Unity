## Why Paete and Phaister read, and she does not

Paete's first art rule (`paete-kit-2026-09-25/direction.md`, section 2) is **"Solid, not glowing"**: his powers are THINGS
(vines, thorns, a tree), toon-lit geometry in his palette, that GROW with an overshoot, HOLD, and WITHER. Phaister's are
things too (the doll, the pins, the marks). Glow is a small accent on both.

Every effect of Amihan's was an abstract translucent strip (`WindRibbon`) lying flat on the court or standing paper-thin,
plus line diamonds. No mass, no object, no silhouette: from any distance it reads as pale lines, and close up as flat
planes ("blocky", "awkward"). Wind is invisible; in every wind design that works, the wind is read by **what it moves**.

## Her object: abel cloth

Her family weaves Abel Iloko in Vigan (her design brief: Binatbatan, cotton beaten free for the loom). So her powers are
**woven cloth carried by the wind**: solid, toon-lit, double-sided strips with the binakol zigzag and abel stripes in her
palette (cream ground, teal, rust, gold), that unfurl, billow, ripple and snap, and end by fraying into cotton and thread.

Rules (one family):
1. **Cloth is solid.** Toon-lit, patterned, opaque, thick enough to cast a silhouette. Wind streaks are thin fast lines
   AROUND the cloth that say speed; they never carry the shape.
2. **Cloth UNFURLS along its length** from where it starts (never scales up from nothing) and **billows**: a travelling
   ripple down its length, its free end whipping.
3. **Force is shown by what it carries.** Cotton, thread, dust, leaves and paper are driven ahead of every front.
4. **Every blow has a hold beat**, then the snap.
5. **Ends by fraying**: the cloth thins to threads and cotton, never by fading.
6. **Readable on Low and on light tiles**: the cloth's dark rust/teal stripes carry contrast on the cream court.

## Per ability

| Ability | The cloth | Force shown by |
|---|---|---|
| Airburst | Off her loom: a giant abel cloth unfurls across the whole half map as the blast front, billowing; the ring round her is a cloth skirt snapping out to 10 m | A cotton storm and street debris driven ahead of it; the thrown players tumbling (`WindTumble`) |
| Whirlwind | A rolling wall of striped cloth, rolling over itself as it travels | Dust kicked up under it, cotton torn into it |
| Drift | A long woven sash streaming behind her, snapping at the turn | Cotton pulled into her wake |
| Featherfall | Cloth panels lifting under her like a kite's, fluttering while she hangs | Cotton rising round her |

Built in order: Airburst (the biggest moment), Whirlwind, Drift, Featherfall.
