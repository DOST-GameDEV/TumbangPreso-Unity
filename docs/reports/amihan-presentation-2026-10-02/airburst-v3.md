## The rethink

**Sentence.** *Amihan calls the monsoon down into her hands, weaves it into her
family's kasikus, and strings the whole lane like a loom; everyone standing in it
feels it coming.*

The live release is the loom's beater: when she drives her palms, the threads beat
home and the whole lane goes at once. The one travelling thing is THREAD: wind
becomes thread in her hands, thread becomes the warp down the lane, and the warp
becomes the fan every player reads in play.

Why the ending cannot show the hit: the cutscene plays before the live 1.5 s dodge
window, so a hit staged here is a promise the rules may not keep. It ends instead on
the REAL players standing in her lane (copied, as Paete's TAKE copies his), bracing
as the first breath of wind passes them, with the warp strung over them. Players
outside the fan stand unbothered. That tells everyone who is in danger and that they
still have time to run.

**Length 5.6 s** (was 3.6; Paete 6.5, Phaister 6.0). Three shots, one idea each,
all on her right side.
