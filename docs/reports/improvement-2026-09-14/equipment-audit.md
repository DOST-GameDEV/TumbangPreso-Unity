launch speed, hit force and post-pickup throwing lock. The same recovery scale
is not yet a meaningful aiming/settling control. All skins share charge duration
and the main bounce/flight response except their trait multipliers/actual shape.

assuming points prove different roles.

Next equipment design: define a useful role/tradeoff for each existing item,
remove strict trait dominance, and connect the differences to perceptible
physical/aim/retrieval behavior. Preserve the iconic balanced default, IDs,
profiles, both modes, truthful descriptions and simple controls. Consider
settling cadence/control for soft/fast-recovery slippers versus heavy impact,
and physically sensible ground bounce/settling. These are candidates, not chosen
or implemented mechanics. Do not add a new economy or grind. Actual can/skin
geometry still contributes; don't judge the whole loadout from numbers alone.
