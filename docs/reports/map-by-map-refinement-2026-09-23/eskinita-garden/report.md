# Eskinita garden refinement, 2026-09-24

Five individually planned tree crowns now leave more space around the retained
house fronts. The before/after pairs at 5_W, 1_E, 5_E and 6_E show the change under
the merged bright look: windows, roof edges and the gap between neighboring crowns
are easier to read. Broad grouped foliage and connected branches remain; the
changes are deliberately moderate. Four other trees are retained unchanged.

The four timber-house planters had inherited their roofs' brown palette. Their
existing leaf triangles now use one small green ramp. Pots, source bodies and
all gameplay collision remain intact. This is a fitted color correction, not a
new shrub model or general recolor of vegetation.

The author verifies unchanged lower 1.4m, root positions and original collider
bounds. Five upper growth meshes are map-local derivatives, with transformed
normals and the established surface-source contract. Four foliage-only overlays
share one material and add 1344 vertices, without shadows or collision. No runtime
behavior was added. Reauthoring reuses original meshes rather than deforming an
already deformed copy.

Existing PrimaryHomesMaterialReview passed 1/1 in 4.882s after authoring; its
unchanged-camera baseline passed 1/1 in 4.801s. Four meaningful matched pairs and
their 25 percent greyscale sheet were personally inspected. Native images for all
ten fronts are retained here, with baseline images in before/. These captures have
the original held arms visible; no claim is made that they are clean promotional
renders. No new tests or capture fixtures were written for this art unit.

The after images expose a separate weakness: the supplied distant mountain painting
reads as oversized pale triangles under the new fog/lighting. Its source artwork
is retained. Next judge its distance and apparent height alongside the whole map
and sky, keeping the urban context and animated clouds. Static cards, bright
