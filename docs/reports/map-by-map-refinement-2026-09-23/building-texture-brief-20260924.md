# Building texture and detail requirement, owner2026-09-24

Exact feedback: "add this to all of map rework idk if uve been adding texture to any building but all buildings feel like they lack in texture and detail".

This applies to EVERY map, including the earlier art batches. Existing shader code,
extra windows, added background buildings and old passing renders do not close this
requirement. Review visible surfaces at actual player, preview/lobby and intro scales
under the merged bright lighting. Describe what is visibly weak, then refine each
building family separately. Preserve good material work; do not add identical noise,
grime, cracks or a shared strength increase across all maps.

- Eskinita: painted masonry and repairs, exposed concrete/block, horizontal timber,
  galvanized or tile roofs, shop shutters/frames/awnings. Check ten near fronts and
  ordinary outer houses, not only the best example. Quiet distance variants still
  need broad roof/wall separation and construction cues.
- Bayan: stone civic construction versus painted walls, timber upper storeys and
  jalousies, roof courses/edges. Keep the church/hall identity and original textures;
  judge their current material readability as well as the sixteen fitted houses.
- Ilalim: concrete formwork/joints versus steel, glass and painted facade surfaces;
  shop shutters/sign mountings/roof seams. Neutralized colours and skyline windows
  are completed improvements, but are not blanket texture acceptance.
- SaBubong: mineral roof deck/walls, coping and drains, sheet shade roofs, tank and
  service materials; near and surrounding apartment facades need readable scale and
  construction detail. Six new neighbours author separate face roles for plaster,
  concrete, metal roofing, glass, timber, cloth and plastic. Review the actual result.
- Lagoon: different timber/bamboo/woven wall construction, roof materials, piles and
  wet/dry contact, railings and lashings. Reference Sama Bajau construction; avoid a
  generic weathered-wood texture pasted on every surface.

For each map, add a building-surface disposition to its report: families inspected,
weaknesses, retained versus refined materials/detail, and actual evidence. Parent
REFINE-2.2 through2.6 remain open for this review plus their integrated camera/native
gates. The work stays sequential and local. No restart of completed unrelated UI,
lighting or capture tooling. Existing research and generated concepts remain inputs,
not proof that a material looks good in the game.
