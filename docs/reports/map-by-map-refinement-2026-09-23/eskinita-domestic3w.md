## Research and interpretation

The UP Sitio Olandes storefront study in the comparative notes supports deliberate
domestic/commercial interface differences, rather than making every opening a shop.
[National Museum basketry](https://www.nationalmuseum.gov.ph/our-collections/ethnology/mats-and-baskets/),
text read, describes local plant-fiber materials and baskets used for household
storage as well as other purposes. A simple household basket is appropriate here;
do not invent/copy a named community's ceremonial weave or imply all baskets are
the same. The[DOST bamboo construction article](https://www.dost.gov.ph/knowledge-resources/news/78-2023-news/3140-local-cross-laminated-bamboo-a-promising-construction-material.html)
was available as an indexed excerpt, while direct open returned500. It supports
local bamboo strips/slats used in furniture/house parts, not this precise blind.
The partially rolled shade and sill arrangement are our practical game-design
interpretation, not a documented reproduction of one traditional house.
