# Filipino house construction and material study,2026-09-23

The owner explicitly renewed the request for texture/detail and Filipino identity
on all houses, with research and generated inspiration before implementation.
This extends the per-map plan; it does not finish the other maps or justify a
global material/noise replacement. Existing final-pass surfaces already provide
19 treatments, so adding another generic shader is not the first step.

## Sources and limits

- [National Museum, Casa Rocha](https://www.nationalmuseum.gov.ph/2022/08/25/casa-rocha-balay-na-tisa-sa-bohol/):
  text read. This Bohol example distinguishes its masonry lower story, timber
  upper story, recessed entry and simpler service wing. Its distinctive roof and
  ornament belong to that particular heritage house. Borrow construction logic
  where appropriate; do not reproduce a Bohol mansion across a contemporary street.
- [UP Diliman, Faustino2025 storefront research](https://digitalarchives.upd.edu.ph/item/64306/7/):
  indexed abstract read; full thesis not accessed.41Sitio Olandes stores were
  classified by full, half, screened/grilled and hybrid openings. The domestic
  and commercial interface supports deliberately different shop thresholds,
  not identical product props on every home.
- [NCCA cultural mapping toolkit](https://ncca.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/WebPosting_Cultural-Mapping-Toolkit.pdf):
  indexed houses/vernacular passage read. Local representativeness matters;
  light-material houses need not be old monuments to carry place identity.
- Prior personally inspected3DAsset Brown City, PEAK and A Short Hike references
  remain in comparative-reference-notes.md. Their useful lessons are frame depth,
  material separation, grouped shapes and quiet readable space, not copying their
  geographic identity, entire buildings or rendering style.

Design inference: strengthen each home's material/construction relationships
and everyday use before adding decorative symbols. This is authored game design,
not an architectural reconstruction or a claim all Filipino homes are alike.

## Generated reference and critique

Built-in image-generation mode, no paid CLI/API. Saved at
ArtSource/map-refinement-20260923/concepts/eskinita-house-materials-v3.png.
Full prompt and provenance live beside it. The actual retained family-b game
study was the style/proportions anchor, not a repaint target.

Keep: medium-scale sheet overlaps, broad quiet plaster variation, fitted timber
courses, substantial window reveals, a supported shade roof and the relationship
between a shop opening/counter and its house. These make surfaces legible without
covering everything in noise.

Reject: the generated left roof ignores the requested non-green preference;
do not use it to recolor existing roofs. The center changes the original stepped
mass and is an idea for an appropriate existing timber family, not a replacement
for family b. Raised gray patches high on the left wall have no clear repair
reason. Do not copy them. The right grille is too fine and its doorway canopy
has a pale artifact. Do not trace either. Foliage rendering is smoother than TUMP.

The image is inspiration, not game evidence. Do not mark houses complete from it.

## First implementation unit: Eskinita primary homes

First inspect the actual ten primary houses at useful near angles. Several already
have jalousies, thresholds, service ledges, timber cladding and laundry. Preserve
good detail, identify what is hidden or too uniform, and avoid duplicating it.
The family-b stepped home is the first target for quiet plaster variation,
readable roof construction and a fitted supported entry shade. Keep its body,
deep frames, original roof color, access and collision. Test its actual appearance
before propagating any material technique to the other individually chosen lots.

Then record a distinct treatment for each primary home and its visible sides,
with roof, wall, opening and household detail separately judged. Extend appropriate
detail to the other Eskinita homes using explicit families/lots, not universal
weathering. Distant silhouettes do not need the foreground texture density.

The other maps use their own construction: civic/heritage relationships at Bayan,
infrastructure and working frontages at Ilalim, roof utilities and neighboring
homes at SaBubong, and locally grounded Sama Bajau timber/pile/boat relationships
at Lagoon. Research their specific next targets before authoring them.
