## 0. Who he is on screen, in one paragraph

Paete is the slowest, calmest thing on the court, and every move he makes should feel like **a tree
deciding to act**. Weight first: the root sinks, the trunk bends, the arms come last (the load), and
then everything is released at once like a branch let go (the snap). He never flails. The world
answers him with GROWTH: things come up out of the ground, grow along it, wrap round what they
catch, and at the end wither back into it. His light is only three things (his eyes, his palms and
the sentry's core), all the same pale yellow-green, so a glance at the screen tells you which of his
things is live.

The references, and exactly what each one gives him:

| Reference | What we take | What we do NOT take |
|---|---|---|
| Groot, Marvel Rivals: Strangling Prison, Vine Strike, walls | the glowing seed thrown, the burst into a bright core with radial spiked vines, bodies held UPRIGHT round it; vines as dark lines, not glowing ones; walls erupting as a burst of trunks | the realistic bark detail and scale; his face acting |
| Kinich, Genshin (Dendro grapple) | the anchor mark, the grapple that always travels, the curved ribbon of the path | the bright particle line (his vine is solid, dark) |
| Pathfinder, Apex; Spider-Man | a short reel with a lift arc, arms locked on the anchor, legs trailing | a real pendulum (too long for a 14 m box) |
| Zyra, League; Yaoyao, Genshin; Maokai | a seed that POPS into a plant, the head-back snap-forward recoil of a plant that shoots, a summon that fights for you | plants that look like creatures |
| Scorpion, Mortal Kombat | catch, a HOLD beat of tension, then the yank | the violence |
| Dead by Daylight | the struggle loop and a bar that fills on the victim's own screen | the horror framing |
| Zarya, Overwatch | the radius read of a pull to one point | floating bodies (ours stand, feet on the court) |

## 2. Art rules for every effect (so they are one family)

1. **Solid, not glowing.** Vines, bark, thorns and leaves are toon-lit geometry in his own palette.
   Only the eyes, the palms and the core glow.
2. **Things GROW, they do not scale.** A vine grows along its own path from its root; a thorn grows
   from its base. Scaling a finished shape from zero is allowed only for pop-ups (the seedling, the
   trunk erupting), and then with an overshoot (`GrowthVfx.Pop`).
3. **Everything ends by withering**: curl, darken, drop leaves, sink. Never fade a solid by alpha.
4. **Every catch has a hold beat** before the pull.
5. **Blocky, cute, readable on Low**: square thorns, stepped trunks, six-sided leaves. No realistic
   bark noise. The can, slippers and players stay readable through every one of these.
6. **Hand-typed, part by part** (the owner's rule): every part its own numbers, never a loop stamping
   one shape round a circle with the same size.
