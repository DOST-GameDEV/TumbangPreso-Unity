## Transfer and change from the plan

The existing heel-load, hip/shoulder lead and catching-foot plan is stronger than copying a magical hover. Add a review distinction between air held close during anticipation and air trailing real displacement. A ribbon moving away before the accepted body movement can make Drift appear to start early. Keep first-person hands away from the central target and derive the world direction from the committed motion, not camera ornament.

Whirlwind's travelling crescent should have an obvious opening behind it. Venti's separated caster and volume is a useful positive benchmark, while 01:25 shows why a complete bright cage is wrong for a 14 metre retrieval court. The gap must survive Low, not merely appear when particle detail is high. Featherfall needs small balance corrections and a grounded catch, with the held slipper remaining visible; floating cloth alone cannot communicate the real airborne state.

Airburst already has the adopted Wiki 1.5 second delay and 60-degree fan. The current plan's roughly 2.5 second movie can remain a presentation sketch inside the shared clock contract. Do not retime the live front from this trailer. A compact opposing arm draw, quiet compression and directed opening is sufficient. Hand back before live response, preserving the actual warning and fan boundary. Do not imply only already airborne victims can be caught or add a full-court cyclone.

## Reject, uncertainty and acceptance

Reject lyre/bow identity, fullscreen flashes, giant vortex cages and camera abstraction. The trailer's lifted enemies prove the photographed moment only, not TUMP force, eligibility or warning rules. The first-person clip cannot establish grounded leg action. No air sound or music was heard; short fabric/air material tests remain listening work.

Acceptance: effects-off motion shows opposite load and a catching foot; departure air follows real accepted travel; cancellation retires preparation; held slipper remains visible through Featherfall; the true Airburst fan catches eligible grounded bodies without phantom hits. Record four overlapping casts at Low and retain directional gaps. The weak point of this reference set is whole-body recovery continuity, so native film must resolve that rather than an extra particle iteration.
