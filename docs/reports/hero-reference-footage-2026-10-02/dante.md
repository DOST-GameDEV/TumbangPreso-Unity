## Transfer and change from the plan

Retain Dante's existing five bands and gameplay geometry. The references sharpen the plan's planted-foot/pelvis/shoulder chain: a small local pressure cue should begin where the body loads the floor, then a visible front travels away, leaving useful ground behind. A simultaneous court-wide dust stamp loses the causal direction and the retrieved shoe. This is a presentation improvement within existing band timing, not permission to add terrain deformation or a new collision sweep.

Use uneven fracture and compact dust around the real contact/band edge. Do not make every band a parallel glowing ruler or every action an orange explosion. The actor's pose should remain legible without rock fragments. For barriers and follow-along structures, avoid filling the near first-person frame; let the field belong to the ground path while arms recover to playable ready. Keep the true hit/miss, approach and remaining structure state readable.

For the ultimate, the existing band sequence needs a short planted personal beat, a release that opens toward the real direction, and handback before live consequences. The entire movie stays under 6.5 seconds. The Breach clip is evidence for progressive clearing rather than a timing template: do not copy its target displacement, through-wall effects, width or launch strength. Cosmetic fragments cannot imply a larger danger region than authority accepts.

## Reject, uncertainty and acceptance

Reject the club, mechanical arms, towering pillars, enormous floating rocks and centre-spanning orange braids. The sampled clips do not prove Itto's exact biomechanics or Breach's opponent view. No sound or ground rumble was heard. Dust appearance reveals no physical simulation or render cost.

Acceptance: with particles off, a reviewer sees ground load before release and a real body recovery; each existing band is recognizable; the strongest eruption leaves the can/shoes locatable; near-ground clutter clears as the front progresses; collision/score remain unchanged. Film caster near cover, target at each band boundary and four-cast overlap at Low. If the hero still reads only through giant debris, fix the pose and smaller field edge before adding effects.
