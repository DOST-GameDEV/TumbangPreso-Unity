## Transfer and change from the plan

Keep Nemu's child-scale intention and Kuro's autonomous companion role. The strongest addition to the existing small-command plan is an attention exchange: Nemu addresses the actual task/location, Kuro orients, then moves. The child should not become a miniature adult combat caster. A head/torso direction or compact simple-hand gesture can carry the intent without a copied wink, new facial detail or enormous spirit manifestation.

Sit's existing ten-second anchor and recast beckon should look different: a settling/attention cue for stay, an actual recall cue for return. Fetch must follow the real shoe and settle near Nemu, not teleport it into a hand. Catch can must remain the existing five-second can-related task and must not imply the child owns a fake can. The independent travel in Seekers supports visible handoff from instruction to world action, while Trailblazer shows the failure mode of taking over the pet's camera.

For Haunt, preserve seen-target, one-at-a-time behavior. Show one honest target handoff and Kuro's real path, not three seeking creatures because Skye's ultimate presents a cluster. A short camera beat can frame child and companion together, then return before the live chase; the total stays below 6.5 seconds. Do not use a giant monster reveal, puppet possession or unobserved victim outcome to fill the movie.

## Reject, uncertainty and acceptance

Reject deity/costume identity, floral court architecture, giant fish mounts, three companion copies and a possessed-camera green vignette. The reference set contains little continuous full-body command-to-recovery footage. No child voice, creature sound or command cue was heard. Any original non-voice attention/response cue needs actual listening and policy-compliant review.

Acceptance: with effects off, Nemu's instruction is legible and Kuro responds as a distinct actor; the actual shoe path and near-child settlement remain visible; recast/expiry produces truthful behavior; camera remains under player control for basics; one-target Haunt does not multiply bodies. Film a missed task, blocked path, interruption and return. A cute isolated creature frame cannot establish companion gameplay clarity.
