## Transfer and comparison with current work

The useful lesson is ordinary scale, credible world location and a bounded transition back to normal play. The visible blue caster-side material is not a reason to turn Hex shoes into purple holograms. Hex deceives a victim about real slipper locations, so real mesh/material appearance and ground contact matter more than a loud magic aura. Do not copy a shoot-to-flash mechanic or make a player pay a new punishment for trying to retrieve a shoe.

The current `phaister-hex.md` already records the scoped refinement result: full authored size with a tiny measured-ground settle, at most three copies, rejected out-of-court/obstructed/offscreen/height-jump/overlap candidates, and view-following hide/restore with normal aging. Natural expiry and teleport cleanup are also covered by the reported native 2/2 lifecycle/placement checks. The real materials and no-shadow tell remain. Do not propose to fix the earlier miniature scale pop or camera view leak again. The earlier outward edge case with no copies was valid cleanup/rejection, not proof of a visible bad placement.

The next useful question is perceptual credibility in representative normal-speed victim play: does a copy feel like another legal slipper at the moment the victim is deciding a route, and can an attentive player reject it without needing high-quality shadows? Static existence and safe placement are necessary but insufficient. Retain the no-shadow tell until actual Low/high comparison shows whether it carries fair information. A close frayed departure is a previously proposed candidate to evaluate only within the authorized Hex scope; this research supplies no native evidence that replacing the current tell is necessary.

Preserve the existing reach, mark, arm, recast, cooldown and 7.5 second Hex status. Also preserve finalized Voodoo, Teleport, Curse Drain, doll/puppeteer ultimate, cameras, models and descriptions. The ultimate remains its authored under-6.5-second presentation, with truthful handback; no new clone montage or forced victim camera is proposed.

## Audio, uncertainty and acceptance

All earlier SFX are reopened, but no audio was heard here. A restrained thread/doll handling cue is a candidate material role, not an observed Yoru sound. Phantom sounds must not impersonate real pickup, can hit/reset, score or tag authority. No compulsory whisper loop, voice imitation, global tint, fullscreen blur or forced rotation is justified.

Acceptance for further Hex evaluation: real victim view with real and phantom slippers, nearby cover, an actual held-source variant, Low/high settings and normal-speed approach; observer view contains no copies; genuine pickup prompts remain truthful; expiry and camera/body changes clean up correctly; failed plausible placement creates no copy. Film before/after only for a coherent demonstrated improvement. The primary uncertainty is human deception/fairness, not whether a renderer exists. The Yoru clip supplies a bounded visual comparison but cannot answer that question for the victim.
