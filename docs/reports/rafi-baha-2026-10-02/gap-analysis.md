## Geometry and safety questions to resolve in implementation

Use the actual map's bounded playable footprint, not one hardcoded radius for
Bayan, Ilalim and Lagoon. A finite wave should reach the far playable boundary
along accepted aim without exceeding published serialization limits. Derive the
needed travel and span from the oriented map rectangle, clip sampled lanes to
cover, and keep conservative live ray checks for narrow obstacles between lanes.
A nine-lane decorative path alone must not authorize contact through thin cover.

Require actual grounded eligibility as well as vertical overlap. The current
height-only test can affect a low airborne jump and does not prove the Wiki rule.
A moved shoe needs its original3m total budget across frames, honest collision
clamping and no second claim by the same field. Carry may require a short bounded
retirement phase after the leading edge passes, rather than truncating shoes at
field expiry. That phase cannot keep the front damaging or hit players again.

The serialized field currently caps radius3, duration3 and endpoint distance9.
Expand only justified bounds and bump compatibility for the coherent batch.
Recording/rejoin must reconstruct the current front and remaining time without
repeating impacts. Preserve consumed-player/shoe identities on repeated snapshots
at the host; clients never resolve gameplay from a recovered visual.
