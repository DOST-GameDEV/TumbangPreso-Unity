## 1. What the reference games do, and what each gives us

Sources marked (general knowledge) were not re-verified frame by frame this session.

| Reference | What happens | What we take |
|---|---|---|
| Genshin Impact, Elemental Bursts (Raiden's Musou Isshin, Zhongli's Planet Befall, Furina's Let the People Rejoice, Kazuha's Kazuha Slash) (general knowledge; wiki as in the earlier research) | a 1.5 to 3 s camera cut: close-up on the face or hands at the INTENT, a pull-back as the power leaves, the world greys or tints behind the hero, the in-world effect lands the moment the camera returns; skipped under water but the timing is kept | the WHO / INTENT / GATHER / RELEASE shape the intros already use, the tint of the world behind the caster, and a fixed timing whether or not the camera plays |
| Honkai: Star Rail ultimates (Acheron, Kafka, Jingliu) (general knowledge) | the screen leaves the arena for a stylised stage in the character's own colours, one hero pose held while the effect builds, then a cut back to the field as it lands | a stage that becomes THEIRS for a beat (already the introduction's stage) and ONE signature held pose per hero |
| Marvel Rivals (Groot's Strangling Prison, Doctor Strange's Eye of Agamotto, Loki's God of Mischief) (general knowledge) | no cutscene: the caster's own body performs in the world with a loud voice line; the effect is readable in third person from anywhere | the LIVE part must read with no cutscene at all (spectators, the victims) |
| Overwatch ult lines (research.md) | enemies hear a threat line, allies a friendly one | the voice lives inside the gesture it describes |
| Street Fighter / Guilty Gear supers (research.md) | a superflash: the world dims and freezes a beat, the character alone lit, then the long cinematic only on a hit | a DIM beat at the release: everything but the caster darkens for 0.12 s |
| League of Legends ult VFX (Riot's readability talks, general knowledge) | the telegraph shows the exact area before it lands; the effect is edges, not fills | every live ultimate draws its true shape at the start (Storm Surge's fan is the precedent) |

## 2. Rules for every ultimate in this rework

1. **Four beats in the cutscene, one pose each:** WHO (the hero turns to camera), INTENT (a
   close-up of the face or hands), GATHER (the power builds in the body), RELEASE (the signature
   pose as the power leaves). Held poses, not drift.
2. **The world is theirs for a beat:** the stage tint and the sky event in the hero's hue.
3. **The live part reads with no cutscene:** victims and spectators see the true area first.
4. **The cutscene never changes the timing:** a blocked camera falls back to the same duration.
5. **The victim's view is designed too:** what the caught player sees and hears, on their screen.
6. **The end is a beat:** the power leaves in a dissipate the player can read ("it is safe now").

## 3. Per ultimate

### 3.1 ABSOLUTE ZERO (Cheska): the whole street stops

- **Idea:** she is the calmest person on the court; the street freezes because she decides it.
  Reference: Genshin's Ayaka and Shenhe (a still, graceful pose while the cold arrives), Star
  Rail's Jingliu (the stage turning to ice behind her).
- **Cutscene (3.4 s):** WHO: she lifts her chin to camera, breath visible. INTENT: close-up of her
  hand closing, frost racing up her sleeve. GATHER: she draws one slow breath, the stage behind her
  frosts from the ground up, snow hangs still in the air. RELEASE: she exhales and snaps her fingers
  down; a ring of frost leaves her across the ground.
- **Live:** the frost ring expands over the WHOLE map in 0.5 s; each body it crosses is encased in
  ice (six-sided facets, her ice hue, the body pose frozen mid-step), Frozen 2.5 s; on thaw the ice
  cracks and falls in plates and a thin frost stays on their shins (Chilled 5 s).
- **Victim view:** frost creeps in from the screen edges, their view stops, a crack sound on thaw.
- **Sound:** a breath in, silence for a beat (the dim), a glassy rushing freeze, per-body ice clamp
  clinks, a staggered cascade of cracks on thaw.

### 3.2 EARTHQUAKE (Dante): the court is his

- **Idea:** the demon of the street stamps and the ground answers. References: Genshin's Zhongli
  (Planet Befall, a meteor from above on a pose), Marvel Rivals' ground slams; Street Fighter's
  superflash dim.
- **Cutscene (3.2 s):** WHO: he rolls his shoulders at camera. INTENT: close-up of his fist
  cracking with gold veins. GATHER: he lifts one knee high, the stage rumbles, pebbles float up
  around him. RELEASE: the stamp; the camera jolts; gold cracks race out of his foot.
- **Live:** cracks race across the whole court from him in 0.6 s, the ground slabs heave (stepped
  stone plates lifting and dropping), every other body is Concussed with a bounce; dust rolls low.
- **Victim view:** the camera shakes hard, the horizon tilts and wobbles for the Concussed time,
  stars circle at the top of the view.
- **Sound:** a low rising rumble, the stamp's sub hit, rolling cracks panned out across the court,
  a dizzy ringing on each victim.

### 3.3 HIGOP (Phaister): the black hole

- **Idea (owner):** *"i want her to really slowly cast the black whole and it can be seen in her
  that power is surging in her and clothes are flying on her"*. References: Genshin's Nahida and
  Wanderer bursts (levitation, cloth driven by power), Overwatch's Zarya Graviton Surge (the pull
  read), Star Rail's Kafka (a slow, relishing cast). Her existing introduction already levitates
  and laughs; it gets rebuilt around the hole.
- **Cutscene (4.2 s, slower than any other on purpose):** WHO: she tilts her head, smiling.
  INTENT: close-up of her palm; a pin floats up out of it and turns point-down. GATHER, the long
  beat (2 s): she rises off the ground, her dress, hair and sleeves whip UPWARD as if falling into
  the sky, pins rise in a slow ring round her, the light bends toward a dark point above her hand,
  the stage goes dim violet. RELEASE: she laughs and throws the point down; it lands and opens.
- **Live:** a dark sphere with a bright violet rim at the aimed spot, an accretion ring of
  stitched thread and pins circling it, the ground dimpling toward it. Every other body and loose
  slipper not hers slides in; a body that pushes away gains a few steps and is dragged back
  (owner's rule). Contact at the centre holds them bobbing in the rim. The end: it collapses to a
  point, a thump, everyone is dropped at the rim.
- **Victim view:** the edges of the screen stretch toward the hole, a low hum grows as they get
  closer.
- **Sound:** a long rising whine under her laugh, the hole's drone, cloth and thread whipping, a
  slipper whistle as each is swallowed, the collapse thump.

### 3.4 KURO PLAYS (Nemu): the pet gets a seat

- **Idea:** Kuro, her ghost pet, is finally let off the leash and joins the game. References:
  summons with a moment of their own (Genshin's Yaoyao's Yuegui, Star Rail's Topaz and Numby), the
  "transformation" beat.
- **Cutscene (3.6 s):** WHO: Nemu winks at camera, Kuro on her shoulder. INTENT: close-up of her
  hand on Kuro's head; she whispers. GATHER: Kuro swells, ink smoke pouring off him, and takes her
  shape (a smoky copy of her silhouette with his face). RELEASE: the copy salutes and dashes out of
  frame.
- **Live:** Kuro walks the court as a player-sized smoky Nemu with his face and a wisp trail, a
  name tag KURO on the scoreboard row, his own slipper; he fades at the round boundary with a bow.
