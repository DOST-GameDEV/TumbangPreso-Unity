  object, owner and8s clock; drop, replacement, role/round exit or expiry retires
  the load. Recovery may bind only the matching held object, never another shoe.
- Keep stable sean_skill2 and FireExplosive wire value, but bump compatibility
  because their gameplay meaning changes. No additional round-charge system.
- The ordinary throw speed and guide remain equal; remove this load's legacy
  speed multiplier. One physical impact consumes the payload once, including a
  wall/can/body/ground hit. Preserve ordinary collision and scoring authority.
- A compact1.25m burst nudges eligible rivals up to1m through existing resolved
  impact ownership. No added status, vertical launch, self-hit or lingering fire.
  Respect solid cover, can protection, tagged players, normal confinement and
  host-only outcomes. Use the existing can knockdown path, never direct score.
- Reuse the existing small ember and Ignition impact visual at the truthful
  radius for this mechanics unit. Dedicated presentation and listened-to SFX are
  separate; do not claim final art quality from numeric tests.
- Preserve the unrelated Flame Rush/Supernova until their own coherent units.
