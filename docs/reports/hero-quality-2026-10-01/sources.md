# External research ledger

Only opened primary material is treated as read. Search snippets are discovery,
not completed research. Historical repository video notes remain separate from
new visual observations. No external assets will be copied into the game.

## Read: competitive visual integrity

[Riot, VALORANT Shaders and Gameplay Clarity](https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/valorant-shaders-and-gameplay-clarity)
explains the tradeoff among art, performance and competitive integrity. Gameplay
information must survive low-quality settings; effect-specific budgets matter.
The article describes replacing costly translucent ability volumes with simpler
outlines and giving vision blockers stable geometry rather than view-dependent
billboards. Transfer to TUMP: keep danger boundaries and authoritative contact
readable at every quality level; spend polish on rhythm/material response, not
on opaque clutter. This is our application of the principle, not a suggestion
to copy VALORANT's lighting pipeline or smoke system.

## Read: sound begins with role and character

[Blizzard, Talent Tree: Sound Designer, Overwatch2](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24226699/talent-tree-sound-designer-overwatch-2)
describes early collaboration among audio, animation, VFX and design, original
Foley experimentation, and distinct first-/third-person needs. Transfer: define
what a cue means to caster, rival and spectator before choosing its source. A
material can be processed creatively without every element becoming the same
synth sweep. TUMP's actual can/footstep/tag information must retain mix priority.
No listening claim is made for embedded videos at this stage.

## Read: meaningful counterplay

[Riot, Champion Counterplay](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/quick-gameplay-thoughts-may-14/)
distinguishes immediate responses from strategic weaknesses. High-impact moments
need proportionate readable responses; an answer should generally not require
owning one particular hero. Transfer: every strong retrieval-denial design must
name a useful response using ordinary movement, throw, retrieve, jump or timing.
Do not overload every small action with a long telegraph, or turn a weakness into
an unwinnable matchup. Proposed TUMP interactions below will be judged by that test.

## Read: identity must survive mechanical translation

[Riot, Illaoi in Project L](https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/dev-illaoi-in-project-l)
uses a character brief, movement studies and prototype guardrails together. The
team rejected an enjoyable early direction because it displaced the fighter's
physical identity with remotely managed entities. Real weighted-prop studies
informed posing. Our application: choose one central verb per TUMP hero, then
reject attractive mechanics that undermine it. Dante's body must cause the fault;
Sean's deliberate release must matter more than an automatic fire field. A good
ability is not merely a fun effect attached to an interchangeable model.

## Read: movement as identity, not a universal dash

[HoYoverse's Natlan introduction](https://blog.playstation.com/2024/08/16/genshin-impact-welcomes-the-sixth-major-nation-of-teyvat-with-version-5-0-going-live-august-28/)
connects three characters to different traversal verbs: drilling, grappling and
surfing. Mualani's described combat ties movement through enemies to her payoff.
Our application: make movement express a distinct decision and body language;
do not give Hydro, Pyro and Electro three differently coloured speed boosts.
Do not import Genshin's resource bars, damage system or traversal scale into TUMP.
This is written-source analysis, not a frame-by-frame combat-film observation.

## Read: silhouette, response and attention hierarchy

[Riot, Clarity in League](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/clarity-in-league/)
treats recognition, effect/hitbox agreement and attention hierarchy as shared
responsibilities across animation, VFX and sound. A telegraph should not promise
more opportunity to dodge than gameplay provides. Lasting cosmetic glow can be
confused with an active power. Transfer: Overclock's permanent identity must be
distinct from its momentary cast readiness; ultimate framing cannot advertise a
live response while controls are frozen. Check facing and effect direction in a
single still, then timing in motion. Extra spectacle in every discipline at once
is not automatically a stronger result.

## Read: timing before expensive detail

[Michael Lyndon, GDC2018 Animation Principles of VFX](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2018/presentations/Lyndon_Michael_ZipThwackPing.pdf),
selected speaker notes/pages17,24,39,52,57,61,64,69,74 were read. Pages64/74 were
rendered and inspected; the first has an empty embedded-media area, the second a
static arch image, so neither constitutes watched motion. The useful method is
cheap key-pose blocking, contrasting timing between layers, and early evaluation
inside the real game. A uniform effect does not improve just by acquiring more
detail. Our application: storyboard Dante's body-to-ground cause and vary the
visual fault cadence without changing authoritative contact timestamps. Cosmetic
debris can settle differently; gameplay bands cannot drift from their hit times.
