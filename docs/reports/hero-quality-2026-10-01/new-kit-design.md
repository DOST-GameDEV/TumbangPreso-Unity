# Missing-kit design workshop

Design proposals, not shipped behaviour. Human anchors are preserved verbatim in
meaning. Values below are initial tuning targets. See sources.md for opened
primary research and reference-audit.md for protected-hero quality lessons.

## Distinction before spectacle

The three kits must answer different questions in the retrieval loop:

- Hydro: can I redirect the route without taking the slipper out of play?
- Pyro: can I commit to one clean release and exploit the opening myself?
- Electro: can I turn objective success into another precisely timed play?

Hydro controls paths, Pyro commits momentum, Electro sequences opportunities.
None gets an automatic tag, unavoidable global disable, damage/health system,
new complicated resource HUD or permanent escape from retrieval. Motion vocabularies
are respectively scoop/fold/release, compress/brace/recoil, and plant/snap/recover.

## Hydro / current Rafi identity

### Passive: Backwash

After manually retrieving the hero's own previously thrown slipper, gain20%
movement speed for1.5s. Once per genuine throw-and-retrieve cycle; dropping and
picking up does not trigger it. Other players' slippers and automatic recalls do
not count. No invulnerability, tag protection or stamina refill.

Why: the reward comes after entering danger, never replaces that journey. A small
water trail points away from the actual pickup; two fading heel strokes make the
short window visible without another meter. Risk: overlaps Amihan's speed fantasy.
Keep it brief and retrieval-specific, rather than a general cast reward.

### Signature: Crosscurrent

Cast a narrow six-metre current that bends the first flying slipper it contacts
sideways along the indicated flow. Preserve speed within a safe validated bound,
thrower/owner identity and scoring credit. One slipper per cast,35s cooldown as a
proposed replacement for the old two-charge model. Cannot affect held/loose shoes,
teleport anything, cross solid cover or reverse a projectile into its thrower
without visibly travelling there. Aim and flow direction must be readable before
contact; use one ordinary aim/release interaction across mouse/pad/touch.

Why retain this idea: it acts on the central object and can spoil or rescue a
throw without replacing it. Counterplay: delay the throw, change its lane or
bait the single-use interception. Risks: aim comprehension and accidental aid to
rivals. First prototype must show the predicted sideways direction honestly,
without restoring the banned full throw-trajectory line.

### Attacking: Skim

Imbue the held slipper for its next throw within8s. At its first ground contact,
it skims forward up to2m along its incoming horizontal direction, then becomes
normally loose. It does not home, automatically equip, pass through bodies/cover
or gain a second scoring event. A body/can impact resolves normally and consumes
the effect.35s cooldown, no held slipper means refusal without spending it.

This is not another Freeze/Concussed payload: it changes where retrieval happens.
The attacker chooses a landing lane; the defender can read the low wake and
reposition. Stop at the same court bounds/geometry as an ordinary slipper. Risk:
a2m skim could trivialize retrieval near the safety line. Cap travel at legal
terrain and test can-side versus outward throws before increasing distance.

### Defending: Water wall (human anchor)

Raise a four-metre-wide, thin water curtain for4s at a point within4m. People can
cross it freely. The first flying slipper crossing loses its forward momentum
and drops on its approach side, then the curtain breaks. It cannot surround the
can, move with the defender, reset it, hide players or block every throw for4s.
35s cooldown. Reject placement through solid cover or outside legal court bounds.

Distinct from Cheska's three-hit solid ice wall and Dante's following barrier:
stationary, single interception, permeable to people, no camouflage. Counterplay:
bait it with one throw, go around it, or move through and throw from the other side.
The central sheet is nearly clear; its top edge and contact ripple communicate
height/width. No thick blue screen over the can.

### Ultimate: Baha (human anchor)

Send one broad, low flood front forward after a readable0.8s live warning. It
travels across the legal court, carrying loose slippers at most3m and nudging each
grounded rival once by up to1.5m. Airborne rivals can clear the front; cover blocks
it. Held shoes, the can's upright state and score are untouched. The caster gains
no automatic retrieval. Initial cost15points. Exact width follows the projected
legal court, not an unbounded circle; host resolves contacts at front arrival.

The spectacle is the street briefly becoming a current, then draining to reveal
where every shoe ended. Avoid a towering tsunami that erases the game. Big payoff
is rearranged retrieval, not a blue earthquake: water transports loose objects,
Dante applies Concussed in sequential faults. Refuse when no legal forward lane
exists rather than spending points on a hidden off-map result.

### Rejected Hydro directions

- Shark mount or surfboard: copies the reference's distinctive identity and adds
  a traversal model unsuited to this small court.
- Free remote retrieve: duplicates Nemu/old Magnet and removes the central risk.
- Mirrorwake as the default new attacker: already exists, but overlaps Phaister's
  deception and leaves the human water/route anchors disconnected.
- Permanent flooded slow floor: excessive shared-area denial and visual clutter.

## Pyro / Sean

### Passive: Steady Ember

Manually retrieve the hero's own previously thrown slipper to ready the next
normal throw's charge25% faster, once within4s. The benefit changes charge time,
not maximum launch speed, impact radius or score. Dropping and picking up does
not refresh it; it is one reward per real throw-and-retrieve cycle. A steady ember
on the held shoe and one quiet click announce readiness, then fade on expiry.

Revision rationale: the first draft required0.6s near-stillness. That rewarded
waiting behind the safety line and was rejected. The revised loop rewards Sean
for re-entering danger and preparing the next deliberate release. Hydro's reward
helps leave the pickup; Sean's helps turn and make another throw. Both depend on
an existing player action, without another counter or farmable resource.

### Signature: Stoke Step

A short committed two-metre grounded burst in the aimed horizontal direction,
with0.18s readable body compression and0.25s recovery. Cannot steer mid-burst,
phase through players/cover, knock down or leave a damaging trail.30s cooldown.
Use the existing movement authority/budget rather than a transform teleport.

Critique: a dash is familiar and overlaps Amihan. Distinction is deliberate
commitment and recovery, not more fire particles; it exists to enter the space
created by a precise throw. Prototype against ordinary sprint before accepting.
If it adds no meaningful choice, cut it rather than layering on a free stun.

### Attacking: Empowered throw (human anchor)

Load the held slipper for one throw within8s. Its first physical impact produces
a compact1.25m pressure burst, nudging rivals up to1m and allowing the ordinary
host can-contact path to determine a knockdown. No damage, Frozen, Concussed or
unconditional point award.35s cooldown; held-shoe refusal is free. One impact,
one payload, one scoring authority. All throw/carry/drop expiry behaviour must
be explicit; charge cannot duplicate through pass/recovery.

Retain the satisfying near-miss identity of Ignition Cannon with a small readable
radius. The existing legacy charge system is a migration, not an extra stack of
uses. Counterplay: move away from the visible landing point or bait an early toss.
A compact core and quick outward dust ring must expose the slipper immediately.

### Defending: Cinder Gate

Place a three-metre ember line within4m after0.35s warning, lasting3s. The first
rival crossing it while grounded receives one short0.8m push back toward their
approach side; the line immediately extinguishes. Jumping clears it. Does not
block throws, can reset, ability casting or an already completed retrieval.
35s cooldown. Owner must choose a route rather than blanket the can.

Distinct from Water wall: affects a body once, never a projectile. Distinct from
Whirlwind: no retrieve/reset lockout. Distinct from ice: no lingering slippery
field. Risk: repeated forced displacement at edges; clamp outcome through normal
confinement and exclude already tagged bodies. No body pinning between obstacles.

### Ultimate: Supernova, proposed retained name with tighter purpose

Sean visibly compresses, launches, and lands at a chosen nearby legal point,
creating one radial pressure release. Initial15points, maximum chosen travel3m,
impact radius3m, one outward impulse up to2m. The can uses its ordinary host
knockdown path if actually in range; no direct AddScore from presentation. No
long-lived burning crater in this proposed revision. Caster commits to the
announced landing point; rivals see the ring early enough to move away.

This is a proposal to revise the existing broad self-centred version, not a claim
it already behaves this way. Its core is a person committing their whole body,
not Dante's travelling field or Zack's permanent power change. The landing's
small bright core expands briefly then clears, leaving normal court visibility.
Do not make a6s movie grant a guaranteed hit: use the shared cinematic gate and
live response window, with outcome sampled only at authoritative landing.

### Rejected Pyro directions

- Another large persistent fire zone: old trail/crater plus line would make every
  button area denial and obscure retrieval.
- Firework barrage with homing missiles: spectacle detached from Sean's physical
  precision and too many independent scoring/contact sources.
- Damage/burn stacks: TUMP has no health combat loop needing that vocabulary.
- Free explosive tag: lets a power replace the defender's actual contact skill.

## Electro / Zack

### Passive: Amped-Up (human anchor, retained)

Each objective point reduces currently running basic ability cooldowns by5s.
Do not reduce ultimate point cost, refund consumed casts, repeat an effect or
bank negative cooldown. Source events come once from authoritative awarded points,
not local celebratory VFX. Clarify score multiplier handling with current scoring:
one awarded objective point is one reduction; duplicate network receipts cannot
apply it again. This design needs cooldowns, so legacy one-charge Magnet cannot
be left half wired beneath the new description.

### Signature: Quick Circuit

Make a sharp two-metre lateral cut chosen by current movement input, or aimed
right if no lateral input.30s cooldown,0.15s tell,0.2s recovery. No trail stun,
teleport, invulnerability or extra speed buff. Direction preview appears before
commit, respecting remapped inputs. Overclock adds a second optional cut within
1s, using the same ability button; it can be declined and expires cleanly.

The second cut is a timing choice, not two mandatory teleports. It cannot refill
itself through Amped-Up or be stockpiled across a round boundary. Risk: overlaps
Stoke Step. Pyro must commit forward into its opening; Electro cuts laterally to
change the angle. Compare silhouettes and tactical traces, not colours.

### Attacking: Bank Shot

Normal: load the held slipper for8s. Its first legal side-wall contact retains
85% of incoming speed (proposed), instead of ordinary45% or first-spin-bank62%.
It still permits only one scoring bank, with no can/body bounce or homing.35s
cooldown. Refuse without a held slipper. Never accelerate beyond incoming speed. A split-arrow icon and one
brief wall contact spark show the ricochet; do not restore the full trajectory.

Overclocked: the same charged throw may retain scoring credit for two banks,
with a clear second contact cue. Each loses at least15% speed, with no added
status. This is an explicit Hero Strike-only exception to the current one-bank
credit limit, not a global Balance change. One throw still cannot score twice
from the same can event; further contacts lose credit normally. This makes the permanent upgrade intelligible
without turning every shot into an unavoidable stun. Host stores remaining banks,
consumption and throw episode; round/impact/expiry clear it. Reject invalid or
unbounded geometry and test corner contacts for duplicate collisions.

### Defending: Closed Circuit

Normal: aim at one visible rival within6m and maintain clear aim/line of sight
for0.4s to apply Zapped for2s. A thin target-facing electrical tell announces the
lock; breaking sight or aim before commitment cancels without a status result.
35s cooldown begins on accepted commitment. No targeting through walls, behind
the caster, on tagged bodies or after the round ends. It never stops ordinary
movement, jump, throw, retrieve or reset; Zapped only blocks abilities.

Overclocked: after a successful contact, optionally acquire a different rival
within the next1s using the same aim-and-press action. That second acquisition
still requires0.4s unobstructed aim and a full visible tell. No automatic chain,
no repeat hit on the first victim, no refresh from Amped-Up during the sequence.
A declined or failed second acquisition simply ends the window.

Revision rationale: the first line-trap draft was too similar to Cinder Gate.
This version asks Zack to expose himself and keep a moving target in view, while
Sean predicts a route. Victims can break sight, cross his aim or keep using the
normal game verbs. Risk:0.4s may be too hard on touch; test aim tolerance and
feedback across devices rather than secretly giving bots perfect locks.

### Ultimate: Overclock (human anchor, retained)

Lightning strikes Zack, applying the Wiki's Zapped status to nearby rivals and
upgrading his abilities for the rest of the match.15points, once per match after
activation; later round reset must retain the upgrade, new match clears it.
Initial strike radius3m and0.8s readable live warning are proposed. It does not
add permanent movement speed, invulnerability, stun trails or extra points.
Persistent state must restore for joining peers and observers without replaying
the strike or cinematic. The shared UI changes the relevant ability descriptions
and distinct icon detail, rather than adding a flashing permanent screen filter.

Snowball risk: a permanent upgrade plus objective cooldown reduction can compound.
Use extra choice/geometry rather than raw numerical buffs, keep it readable, and
measure objective gains before raising strength. Do not silently replace the
owner's match-long duration with the old7s Thunderstrike.

## Unresolved critique before Wiki publication

1. Pyro and Electro still have short movement actions. Their commitment and
   optional direction-change loops must pass a silhouette/decision comparison;
   different colours are insufficient. Do not add overlapping trail disables.
2. The camping passive and duplicate line trap were rejected and replaced above.
   Keep these rejected alternatives as history, not a second implementation.
3. Hydro's object relocation must not send every shoe to safety. Test directions,
   endpoints and maximum per-object travel before acceptance.
4. None of these ideas is visually proven. Film-reference access is incomplete;
   the animation plan must record that gap rather than claim reference parity.
