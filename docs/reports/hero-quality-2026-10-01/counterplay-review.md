# Paper counterplay review and chosen revisions

This is reasoning over current rules, not a playtest or balance measurement.
Prototype values remain proposals. It is intended to catch obviously weak ideas
before they become expensive animations or misleading Wiki promises.

## Four-player situations

### Can upright, one shoe loose beside it

Hydro still has to enter for the pickup. Backwash only starts after the genuine
manual retrieval, so it cannot protect the approach. Baha can relocate the shoe
but announces travel and never equips it. Defender can reposition or wait for the
front rather than being forced into an automatic tag/untag outcome.

Sean can use a short committed step to enter, but its recovery leaves a response
window. Empowered throw requires actually holding a shoe; it cannot be a remote
rescue button while empty-handed. Cinder Gate belongs to the defender role and
cannot be combined with Sean's attacker slot in the same role state.

Zack's bank keeps the thrown object in play, unlike old global Magnet recall.
Closed Circuit blocks powers but leaves manual retrieval and movement available.
Overclock adds optional follow-ups; it does not grant a free completed retrieve.

### Two attackers approach from different sides

A one-use Water wall answers one incoming shoe, not both lanes. A one-use Cinder
Gate answers one grounded crossing, not the entire circle around the can. Normal
Closed Circuit requires tracking one target in sight; Overclock's second target
requires a new visible acquisition and a different victim. These are materially
different decisions despite all serving defense. No field may be silently made
can-centred to compensate for a missed placement.

### Can down, defender trying to reset

Water wall does not protect/reset a down can. Skim and Baha may move loose shoes,
not the can. Pyro's burst can only affect can state through the existing legal
impact path; it cannot award a new point for an already-down can. Closed Circuit
must not block ordinary reset just because it blocks abilities. All ordinary
status-immunity and already-tagged rules remain applicable.

### Tight corner or irregular obstacle

A displacement is clipped through existing legal movement/confinement, not a
teleport through geometry. No repeated push pins a body: gates are one-use and
Baha keeps one-contact identity. Bank Shot must deduplicate a single corner hit,
lose energy and cap credited banks; an invisible confinement boundary must have
an honest readable contact cue if it is used as a bank. Do not add physical walls
or change map architecture to make a proposed ability look useful.

### Low graphics, sound off, touch input

Clear edges and body direction remain. Audio adds confidence but cannot be the
only warning. Closed Circuit acquisition needs a visible break and a tested aim
cone; do not silently use perfect target lock. Charge and recast prompts read
bindings, never a hardcoded key. Optional second actions expire without another
mandatory tap. The new hollow-circle reticle may carry truthful state but must
not become the only indicator of another player's dangerous cast.

## Decisions accepted for proposed Wiki copy

- Keep the explicit human anchors. Author missing behaviour as proposed design,
  not an assertion about the current player.
- Reject stationary Steady Ember: reward a real retrieve instead, one per throw.
- Reject duplicate Electro ground gate: use exposed, breakable aimed acquisition.
- Keep a basic movement action on Pyro and Electro only with different commitment
  loops. Pyro has a short windup/locked burst/recovery. Electro is a quick cut with
  an optional separate second action after Overclock. Neither has a passive stun
  trail. They must pass a real decision/silhouette comparison before final tuning.
- Correct Bank Shot's description: everyone already banks. The new benefit is
  bounded energy retention and, while Overclocked, one extra credited bank. It
  does not create unlimited ricochets or change Classic's bank/scoring limits.
- Keep parol construction and casual electrical confidence as existing strengths.
  Do not mistake replacing those with generic explosion poses for improvement.

## Still requiring implementation evidence

Cooldown/point economy over eight rounds, touch aim tolerance, long-term Overclock
snowball, Hydro's ability to move shoes toward safety, and how often short movement
skills replace ordinary sprint. No numeric pass rate or quality score is invented.
Use representative actual play after focused correctness tests. Reject a feature
that removes the retrieval decision even if its VFX looks excellent.

External motion access remains incomplete. Written primary research, inspected
native historical contact sheets and a critiqued generated storyboard support
these plans; none is a substitute for watching the new native implementation.
