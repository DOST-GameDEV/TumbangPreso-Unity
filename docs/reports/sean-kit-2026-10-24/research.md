## The rules this gives Sean's ultimate

1. **The cutscene is the change, the state is play** (Itto, Varesa, Lapu-Lapu). It should not show the bomb landing: that
   happens live, later, aimed by him. It ends on the awakening blast hitting the real players beside him (the Paete rule: end
   on the effect landing on people).
2. **Fist in front, face behind** (Wriothesley, Lighter), then **looking up at him** (Zangief): the two brawler shots.
3. **One thing travels: the heat**, always upward, from the court into his feet, up his legs and arms into his fists and eyes,
   and out as the eruption. Vertical light is power (2026-09-24 vocabulary).
4. **The emblem is Mayon's cone**: a near-perfect cone with concave sides and a lit summit. One shape, drawn where the power
   moves (behind him, under him, in the eruption's column). It is a mountain's silhouette, not a sacred symbol.
5. **The backdrop steps back** to his world (the Jujutsu domain idea in the earlier research): the mountain on the horizon
   behind the court, dimmed and warm. Return the daylight at the hand-back.
6. **Never white**: the hottest colour is a yellow-orange core, the crust is near-black basalt, the glow is orange.
7. **Short beats, held poses**: the brawlers spend 0.3 to 0.6 s on each pose and cut fast between them.
