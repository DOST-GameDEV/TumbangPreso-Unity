## 4. The owner's complaints, answered by the footage

1. **"her yellow circle looked weird as fuck when she was floating".** In none of the seven wind characters is there a
   ring floating under the feet of a body in the air. Height is shown by the SHADOW on the ground (Wanderer), and the wind
   holding a body up is drawn round the LIMBS (swirls at the hands and shins) and as streaks rising past the body. A flat
   ring glued to the soles reads as a disc she stands on, and a bright one reads as a UI marker. Rule: **nothing flat
   under her feet in the air**; a small dust swirl ON the court below her, turning, is the ground's answer.
2. **"she didnt have a float animation".** The footage's floats are ALIVE: knees tucked unevenly, a lean into the travel,
   cloth and hair lifted UP, small corrections, arms out for balance while moving and drawn in while still. Rule: a
   hand-keyed flight loop with its own beats (a settle, a breath, a correction, a turn), and separate poses for rising,
   hanging still, drifting, throwing from the air and coming down.
3. **"any VFX to indicate her shit".** Every reference shows the wind DOING the work: a disc pressing the ground, a column
   of streaks, a tube round a dashing body, bodies lifted and turned in a gale. Rule: **the wind is visible wherever it
   acts on a body** (hers or anyone's) and the court shows it (dust pressed out, dust dragged along).
4. **"visually show the wind actually assisting her"**: the same rule, from her side. The dash is a gust from BEHIND
   that pushes her (a crescent at her back, the tube round her); the flight is a column UNDER her that she rides; the
   landing is the column folding down and the dust puffing out.
