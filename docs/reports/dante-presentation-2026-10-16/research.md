## 2 · Critique: what the reference cutscenes get wrong for TUMP

1. **Length.** HSR runs 8 to 12 s. That works when only the caster is waiting. In TUMP the pause stops four players, often
   several times a match. Genshin's 2 to 3 s is closer to right; Basilio's agreed 7.0 s is already the longest in the cast and
   must earn every second.
2. **White-out.** Itto at 2.9 s and Aventurine at 6.3 s lose their subject in a bright flash. TUMP keeps saturated hero colour
   and never white (`AbilityShowcaseProbe`'s 12 per cent blowout rule, kept in spirit).
3. **Effects instead of acting.** The weakest frames are particle storms where the body has stopped moving. The best frames
   (Zhongli's arm sweep, Breach's brace) are a body pose CAUSING the effect.
4. **The payoff off screen.** Zhongli's cutscene ends before the meteor lands. For Basilio the owner chose this deliberately
   (end on the fist), so live play must carry the payoff with real scale; it is the most important 2.4 s of his kit.
5. **Too many emblems.** Itto's crest is great once. Shown on every beat it becomes wallpaper.

## 3 · The rules this gives Basilio

1. **His body causes everything.** Feet, fists and shoulders press, and the court answers. Nothing appears from the air.
2. **Light lives inside the stone.** Molten orange only in cracks and seams, cut in (a hole, never a raised ridge). Basalt
   stays solid and flat shaded. His jade is for UI and his ward glyph only.
3. **Weight is a held beat.** Every cast has a short compression before the release, and heavy things move in short jolts,
   then hold. Big things move little.
4. **Endings cool, then crumble.** Seam orange to dull red to grey, then the stone cracks, drops and sinks. Never alpha fade
   a solid.
5. **One silhouette per skill.** Unstoppable is vertical (him standing tall), Boulder is a compact mass in his hand, Bastion
   is a horizontal wall, Continental Drift is a forward line that branches.
6. **The tell is the counterplay.** The bigger the effect, the clearer its warning: a fuse runs ahead of Drift, Bastion
   rises in sequence, a loaded Boulder is readable from across the court.
7. **Status effects are felt, not only listed.** Concussed gets a screen effect and a sound on the victim, like Breach's.
