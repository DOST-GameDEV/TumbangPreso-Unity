## Refined shared gesture

The body finishes the accepted step according to the actual hand shortfall,
with at most50cm of extra render-only displacement. It never steps backward to
undo a valid close gesture. The arm remains limited to10percent extension.
Far quick tags use28degrees of chest lean and42degrees of shoulder turn instead
of driving the large head forward with38degrees of lean. The visual palm target
is inset from the padded gameplay capsule. Lunge's44degree dive is retained.
Every offset restores; the motor, tag authority, scoring and gameplay reach are
unchanged. No model, asset or hero-specific kit is edited.

The first adaptive-step experiment improved contact but could subtract the close
step, and still left8.5cm at Nemu's skin. It was not shipped. Forward-only stepping
and the refined target/posture resolve those failures. The old far-lean assertion
specified the previous38degree pose; it now requires25degrees for the intended
28degree pose. Contact, skin proximity, arm cap, timing, recovery, motor and score
assertions were not loosened.
