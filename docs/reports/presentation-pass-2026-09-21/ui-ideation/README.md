## First generation prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Produce ONE polished art-director ideation contact sheet for a small stylized 3D Filipino street sports game called TUMP. Three large landscape in-game mockups stacked vertically, each at 16:9, with a tiny A/B/C label outside the game viewport. Reference image 1 is the ACTUAL GAME ENVIRONMENT AND APPROVED BLOCKY CHARACTER STYLE: preserve its 3D court, chalk boundaries, tiny can, Filipino street, cute blocky people and lighting faithfully. Reference image 2 is ONLY an energy/typography inspiration photograph of Sepak U, NOT an edit target: do not reproduce the monitor, hand, YouTube page, or Sepak U branding. Design three genuinely distinct compact HALFTIME POPUP directions over the court, not fullscreen pages. At least 80 percent of every gameplay viewport remains visible and undimmed. No green in any UI treatment. No green background, no olive panels, no fullscreen wash, no big face occupying half the viewport, no menu buttons, no sidebar dashboard, no glossy sci-fi HUD. The halftime UI should feel like a playful authored sport announcement appearing briefly, clearly part of the game, then withdrawing automatically. It must be implementable as a responsive animated Unity overlay. Each design has one compact composition near the lower portion of the viewport, with a strong 'HALFTIME' headline, a concise standings strip for four seats ('P1 MARING 140', 'P2 PLAYER 95', 'P3 TIKBOY 80', 'P4 LOLA 70'), and small 'NEXT TAYA: P1 MARING' / 'ROUND 5 IN 4s' orientation. Use restrained small character/seat details, legible large scores and purposeful asymmetry. Show the composition at actual in-game scale, NOT an enlarged isolated UI asset. A: warm oxblood/coral red, chalk white and charcoal, bold cheerful hand-painted sports lettering, small slanted red tab with a clean light standings strip beneath, subtle authored uneven edges. B: energetic red, sun-yellow and warm white, punchy anime-sports broadcast typography, offset compact score tickets with clean spacing and a strong next-taya tag; inspired by the reference's excitement without copying its layout. C: ink charcoal, bright cool blue and warm white, more restrained sports broadcast ribbon with a playful chunky headline and four compact seat/score chips; no generic dashboard cards. Different silhouettes for all three, not just recolors. Strong composition, readable text, cohesive typography that suits the existing cute low-poly game. The arena and players stay the main visual content. No extra copy, no instructions or implementation annotations inside the game viewport.
