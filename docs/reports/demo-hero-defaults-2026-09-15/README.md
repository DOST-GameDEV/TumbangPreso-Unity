# Existing default hero skills: presentation baseline

One focused PlayMode case passed across all six heroes and18default slots. Every
slot accepted the real press/release, and each declared body action appeared in
its recorded frame trace. The body and first-person action IDs are distinct across
the18 slots. Moving sprint, moving stone protection and empowered throws were
included. Lingering fields were allowed to expire between isolated presentations.

The test deliberately supplied ultimate charge and marked the reviewed actor as
a bot, so no player's unlock history was written. This is a controlled presentation
and input-path review, not proof of natural charge acquisition or every counterplay
interaction. Alternate loadouts remain separately scoped work.

Observed ultimate staging is distinct: Sean prepares a leap/landing, Dante drives
a deep ground action into the fissure, Zack summons the strike, Cheska braces for
the radial burst, Nemu raises the seance into the preserved monster, and Phaister
draws out the eclipse ritual. A still at0.65s showed Zack idle, but its actual summon
clip runs0.7829..1.5601s in the recording; no missing-animation fix was warranted.

Visual critique and limits:

- Nemu's current monster reads clearly from the owning camera and the new block
  palms remain intact. This did not modify the monster or familiar artwork.
- Dante retains the clean hooked stone forms and selective seams. The elevated
  witness view can have foreground laundry in the composition; that is real scene
  dressing, not removed for a cleaner screenshot.
- Sean's large ember chips can look coarse in close views, and Phaister's floor
  ritual is visually dense. These are saved polish candidates after the tested
  demo path, rather than a reason to replace the approved whole skill designs.
- Still frames and acceptance flags are not owner artistic approval. Review the
  ordinary-speed clips and test actual opponents, overlap and interruption later.
