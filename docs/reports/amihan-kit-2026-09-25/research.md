## 2. What each taught

### Genshin, Venti (Anemo, the closest fiction to hers: a wind bard)

Observed, 179.6 to 190 s: idle, then **a 0.6 s push into a Dutch-angle close-up** of his face
and hands drawing a bow made of wind, the world behind graded dark teal with **white speed
streaks** radiating across frame and one line of voice ("Time for takeoff!"). Cut to **a low wide
shot**: he is small in frame and a spiral of **thin white crescent blades** sweeps round him
under a darkened sky. Cut back to the gameplay camera, where the Stormeye opens as a **dark eye
with bright teal orbital rings**, not a fog volume.

- The close-up is short and the pose is the whole message.
- Wind is drawn as THIN BRIGHT EDGES (crescents, rings, streaks) around a DARK centre. Nothing is
  a filled translucent blob.
- The grade does the "stage": the world goes dark and teal behind a lit character.

### Genshin, Kazuha (Anemo)

Observed, 207 to 212 s: the stage fills with **long horizontal ribbons of wind at several depths**
(near, middle, far), **maple leaves** tumbling in front of and behind him, a bright horizontal
slash line across the frame, and a teal wash over the ground. The leaves are the only warm colour.

- Wind needs a PARTICLE MOTIF that is the character's own (his leaves). Amihan's is the weave:
  cotton fibre and abel threads, and the kasikus whirlwind pattern on her back.
- Depth layering (ribbons near, mid, far) is what makes a flat effect feel like a place.
- One warm accent against the teal wind is what the eye follows.

### Genshin Anemo from a far fixed camera (the effect without the camera)

Observed across the 63 frames: every Anemo burst reads from far away as **a bright ring or
crescent on the ground with a darker core**, sparse single-pixel sparks, and **thin arcs
orbiting a dark eye** (Venti at 38 to 46 s). The ground rings never cover the whole field and
fade by thinning their edge, not by dimming a disc.

- From gameplay distance, readable wind = a bright rim and a motion direction.
- Fade out by thinning the line, not by alpha on a filled area.

### Honkai: Star Rail, Feixiao (wind)

Observed: **establishing wide** of her and the target; **an extreme close-up of her eye**
(the Mario Strikers "eyes" beat, again); a leap with the camera tracking; strikes; the finale is
**a huge swept vortex of wind** that fills the frame, then a freeze on the impact and the
aftermath tornado.

- A good ult cutscene has four beats: **who** (wide), **intent** (eye/hand close-up), **power
  gathers** (the element builds around a signature pose), **release** (the power leaves, the
  camera follows it out).
- The finale is the element at a scale the gameplay never shows.

### Valorant (written sources: Dexerto, Dot Esports, the Valorant wiki)

- Every ultimate has **two voice lines**: an encouraging one allies hear and a warning enemies
  hear, so each cast is fairly announced. The line is two or three words of personality.
- No cutscene: the ult is FAIR because it is loud. The audio tells everybody where and what.
- Jett's Blade Storm: the knives appear with **white air swirls**; the third-person silhouette
  changes so enemies can read it from range.

## 3. The rules this gives TUMP (used in [direction.md](direction.md))

1. **Four beats**: who, intent, gather, release. Under 4 s total (TUMP pauses all four players).
2. **One close-up**, on the eyes or hands, with speed streaks: it is the "this is an ultimate" beat.
3. **The world grades down behind a lit character** (dark teal-green for her), and comes back.
4. **Wind is bright thin edges around a darker middle**: crescents, rings, ribbons, streaks.
   Never a filled translucent disc.
5. **Depth layering**: ribbons near, mid and far.
6. **A motif particle that is hers**: cotton bolls and abel threads; the kasikus pattern.
7. **One warm accent** (her gold brooch, the cream cotton) against the green wind.
8. **The finale is the element at a scale gameplay never shows**, then the camera hands back
   to the live ability at the exact pose it continues from.
9. **Voice is short, inside the gesture, and differs for allies and enemies** (Valorant).
10. **Fade by thinning the line, not by dimming a disc.**
