# Terms content and presentation,2026-09-23

Owner explicitly requested a longer, better Terms popup and a filled-square consent
control in login. This is the narrow exception to protected login art. Expanded the
existing four short play/account guidelines into13readable sections using the actual
account, guest/local-save, multiplayer, report, progression and soft-currency scope.
Preserved the truthful optional-local-email/no-email-password-recovery notice.
No invented support address, registered company identity, retention deadline, refund
promise, paid purchase system, guaranteed moderation or statutory-compliance claim.
This is product drafting, not a certification of legal sufficiency; operator/contact
and any release-specific privacy notices need the owner's review before public release.

Primary references checked:
- https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-faq : Unity distinguishes
  player-facing privacy information and service-dependent data processing. The copy
  points at the player policy rather than claiming one fixed SDK data inventory.
- https://docs.unity.com/services/data-safety-requirements : disclosure must reflect
  actual integrated services. No blanket claim that accepting play terms supplies
  every separate permission a platform or law may require.
- https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/v15BRedacted5D20Advisory20Opinion20No.202019-007.pdf : transparency requires meaningful information about processing
  and rights. No unsupported timeline or legal waiver was invented to make text longer.

UI: OwnerTermsView keeps a bounded warm-dark reading surface, clear section hierarchy,
real masked scrolling and visible scrollbar, fixed BACK/I AGREE actions and focus
inside the dialog. Opening/BACK must not change consent; acceptance fills the original
form toggle. No forced scroll-to-bottom or default acceptance. Actual rendering and
mouse/pad/touch checks still pending; source wording alone is not completion evidence.
