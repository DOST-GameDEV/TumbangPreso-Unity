# Gameplay trailer: draft flow

Draft 1, 2026-11-03. The owner's track `TUMP TRAILER.wav` is the master: picture
follows its beat grid and the audio is never retimed. A reference copy for timing is
`ArtSource/trailer/tump-trailer-reference.mp3`. Prompts for every animation and cutscene
shot: [TRAILER_PROMPTS](TRAILER_PROMPTS.md).

At 24 fps a beat is exactly 9 frames and a bar 36. At 60 fps put cuts on the
nearest frame.

## References

- Dodgeball Academia launch trailer (Nintendo of America): text cards between
  gameplay bursts, a character lineup beat, a run of super moves into a white
  flash and the logo.
- Sepak U gameplay trailer (IGN): cold open on a Philippine street, nearly all
  real gameplay, in-game callouts doing the work of text cards, a gag after the logo.

This draft takes Sepak U's street open and gameplay share and Dodgeball Academia's
escalation: the rule, the heroes, the places, then the ultimates into the slam.

## Mix

48 shots. Gameplay 67%, ultimate cutscenes 15%, UI motion 10%, character
animation 2%, black/flash/logo 6%.

## Capture

- Play Hero Strike bot matches on each of the six maps; each match saves a replay.
- Shoot gameplay from Menu, Replays: free camera, P1 to P4 follow, 0.25x to 4x
  speed, H hides the interface. Record at 60 fps. See [local replays](LOCAL_REPLAYS.md).
- Use the replay's own 0.25x and 0.5x for slow motion rather than frame blending.
- Capture ultimates live. Ultimate visuals in replay are still open (TAG-PHASE-1031).
- Map films ([method](MAP_FILM_METHOD.md)) can supply the cold open plates.
- Text cards: Darumadrop One, chalk white with the black in-game outline, no blue.

## Edit rules

- Every cut on a beat; section changes on the phrase lines.
- Game sound only on marked hits, about 12 dB under the music.
- Where the kick thins, slow the picture down or open it wide.
- 30 s cutdown: shots 1 to 4, then 27 to 48.
