WHY THIS ONE
------------
🧑 asked for a pad "tilted like this ps5 controller", and the tilt is the whole point: on a flat
front view the shoulder buttons are edge-on, so L1 and L2 are one visible bar and the map has to
put two labels on one shape. This drawing shows the TOP FACES of both shoulder buttons at each
top corner, stacked, which is exactly the cue the reference picture has. The author says why in
their own readme, and it is the same reason this screen exists:

    "I made those vector gamepad out of my need for having gamepad settings in my own game,
    Past Future, and what I found on the web doesn't have back trigger buttons visible."

WARNING  IT IS CC0, WHICH MEANS THE PREVIOUS ART'S CREDIT OBLIGATION IS GONE.
This replaced Tokyoship's "Dualshock 4 Layout" (CC BY 3.0), which shipped for part of one day and
required a credits line. CC0 requires nothing. ⚠️ **The line was MOVED, not deleted**: it is in
`CreditsContent.CourtesyCredits` now, which is the array for sources that ship without owing
attribution, because the author asked nicely for a shoutout and that costs one line.

WARNING  THERE IS NO TRADEMARK IN THIS ONE, AND THAT IS WORTH SAYING BECAUSE THE LAST TWO HAD ONE.
Both previous sources drew Sony's mark and `build_controller_diagram.py` carried a disc-erase step
to remove it (a licence to reuse a DRAWING is not a licence to the trademark inside it; see
`docs/Port_Plan.md` § 8 on the IKE slipper). This drawing's centre button is a plain circle and
its body carries no wordmark, so that step is gone. **If the source art is ever swapped again,
check for a mark before assuming there is none.**

WARNING  IT IS RECOLOURED ON THE WAY IN, LIKE EVERYTHING ELSE SOURCED INTO THIS PROJECT.
The body is neutral grey, the keylines are white, and the face glyphs are cyan, pink, red and
blue. `CLAUDE.md` § 6.4 bans cold grey as flatly as it bans navy, and two of those four glyph
hues are illegal here outright. Every pixel is mapped onto the warm ramp by luminance, and the
saturated marks are flattened to one honey so the four buttons are told apart by SHAPE, which is
`docs/FUTURE.md` § 16.1's rule.

WARNING  THE COMMITTED PNG IS AN INPUT, NOT A CONVENIENCE.
Every leader line on the CONTROLLER MAP screen is measured in the coordinates of THIS file, so
the tool refuses to run if its size changes. It is the pack's own 2048 px render cropped to the
drawing's alpha bounding box plus six pixels, which is 1698 x 1078. The SVG is kept beside it for
provenance and for anyone who wants to re-render it; re-rendering means re-measuring.
