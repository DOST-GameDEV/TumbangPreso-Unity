WHY IT MAY BE IN THIS REPOSITORY
--------------------------------
`docs/Asset_Sourcing.md` section 1 rule 1: every listed asset costs zero, and paid,
subscription, trial, non-commercial, GPL and CC BY-SA assets are excluded. CC0 is none of
those. Rule 8: "CC0 and CC BY source may live in this public repository with the proper
licence." This file is that licence.

WARNING  IT IS NOT USED AS BOUGHT, AND THAT IS NOT OPTIONAL.
The source art is near-black with GREEN, MAGENTA, RED and PURPLE face buttons.
`CLAUDE.md` section 6.4 bans blue, navy and cold grey in any UI layer, and section 139.4
records a MAGENTA tick shipping in the settings panel as a defect worth its own table row.
`tools/build_controller_diagram.py` recolours every pixel onto the warm brand ramp on the way
in, exactly as `tools/build_input_glyphs.py` does for the bought glyph sheets and for the same
reason its header gives: a pack dropped in as bought puts colours nobody chose on the one
screen a player reads.

WARNING  THE COMMITTED PNG IS AN INPUT, NOT A CONVENIENCE.
`build_controller_diagram.py` reads the PNG rather than the SVG, and the SVG is kept beside it
for provenance only. The reason is the anchor table: every leader line on the CONTROLLER MAP
screen is measured in the coordinates of THIS rasterisation. Re-rendering the SVG with a
different engine (Inkscape, resvg, a browser, macOS `qlmanage`) moves sub-pixel edges and,
worse, may pad or crop the canvas differently, which would silently shift all eighteen anchors.
The raster is the contract. Regenerate it on purpose, and re-measure if you do.

The PNG was produced on macOS with:

    qlmanage -t -s 2400 -o . ps3_gamepad_cc0.svg

and then cropped to (0, 0, 1960, 1240), which is the drawing plus its own margin and none of
the square preview canvas Quick Look pads it into.
