# Cheska GLACIAL WALL, g6 DepthB: approved 2026-10-18

Owner, after the g6 close-ups and motion film: "depth b looks good".
DepthB is the in-between painted ice (4 value bands, cyan, frosted rim, four-point glints, climbing shimmer) with
stylised depth: a dark core where the crystal is thick, bright fracture veins at three depths sliding under the
surface, a cloudy inner volume and a frosted shell at thin edges. On the approved crystal shapes (`IceCrystalKit`).
`CheskaGlacialWallLook.Build` now defaults to `Style.DepthB`. Look only; not yet hooked into a match
(`CheskaGlacialWallLook.Enabled` is false and nothing in the game calls Build yet).

Kept as filmed: the look class, its shaders (`CheskaWallBetween` is the DepthB surface), the film tool, the motion film
and the close-up sheet. Owner notes that led here: g2 Crystal "too realistic"; Painted/Chunky/Blocky "all look the
same"; "give me an in-between texture design ... slightly more cyan"; g5 "still lacking the depth detail the
realistic one had".
