read as cyan and the owner had them changed ("make it more green, and add some hueshifts with green and blue as
extremes"), then approved the court film ("looks good"). Asked about the bench, he released its colour:

> "it doesnt really matter waht color the bench is. but maybe apply hueshifts to the bench too"

only the colours changed: the same shapes, horn mix, tapes, ground, motes and motion. Against h8 in luminance the
worst frame differs by a mean of 2.06 of 255, with the lit area in the same place and 4 to 5 percent smaller (the new
colours are deeper). `whirlwind_h8/` stays as the record of what he first approved.
