# BOULDER's edge wear, the fine noise toned down a little (approved 2026-10-17)

The owner, of the three-column board `danteboulder_edge_wear_nobloomr1.jpg` (as first approved | toned down a little |
toned down more): **"use the toned down a little one"**.

What changed, and only this: `FxStudyDanteBoulder.Stone()` sets `_Noise` to (0.55, 0.50, 0.60, 0). The edge wear's
fray is read at 0.55 and its grain at 0.50 of their first frequency (broader strokes, fewer specks) and the grain
bites at 0.60. Band 6 to 22 mm lifting 38 percent, shoulder lift 6 percent, line 1.5 to 5.5 mm, swell, cuts in the
line, the band's broken inner side, the rocks, the bakes and the molten light are all as approved in `../` (c3).
The world's six-rock stone, his hand's four-rock stone and the rubble all take it (one material function).

The board's cancelled inner-edge override (`_Wear.w`) was taken out of the shader the same day: he said the inner edge
"should NOT become a clean boundary, it should cut off in some areas".
