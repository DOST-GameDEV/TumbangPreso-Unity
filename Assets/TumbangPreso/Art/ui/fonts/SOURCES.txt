THE TWO FACES THIS GAME IS SET IN, AND WHAT THEIR LICENCE IS

This repository is public and the game is a competition entry, so every face here is under
the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and the licence text is committed beside it. Neither font is
modified in outline: Darumadrop is the upstream release byte for byte, and Familjen Grotesk
is a static instance cut from the upstream variable font at wght 400 and wght 700 (see the
note at the bottom, and scratchpad/fontsrc/instance2.py).

docs/TODO.md section 133 is the work that added the second one, and it says why there are
two rather than one.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Darumadrop One, by Maniackers Design
   https://github.com/ManiackersDesign/darumadrop
   SIL Open Font License 1.1

   THE DISPLAY FACE. Headings, the Display and Title steps of PaperKit's type scale, the
   primary action on a screen, hero and player names. It is a rounded high-personality
   face and it is exactly right on a word somebody LOOKS AT.

   It ships ONE WEIGHT, which is the whole reason for the face below.

2. Familjen Grotesk, by Familjen Sthlm
   https://github.com/Familjen-Sthlm/Familjen-Grotesk
   SIL Open Font License 1.1, OFL-FamiljenGrotesk.txt in this folder
   Regular (wght 400) and Bold (wght 700), instanced from the upstream variable font

   THE SUB FACE. Sentences, captions, hints, placeholders, chat, ability descriptions:
   everything somebody READS rather than looks at. PaperKit.FaceFor's boundary is Caption.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
IT REPLACED NUNITO ON 2026-09-03, AND THE REASON IS THE ONE MEASUREMENT THE FIRST PASS
DID NOT USE

Nunito was chosen on 2026-09-03 for having the x-height closest to Darumadrop's. It shipped
for one afternoon and was rejected by eye: "i think our sub font sucks its too ai".

That is a fair reading and the numbers say why. X-HEIGHT ALONE IS THE WRONG TEST FOR A
PAIRING. It answers "does a row shrink when it moves to the sub face", which matters,
and says nothing about whether the two faces look related. The measurement that answers
THAT is the ratio of x-height to cap height, and Darumadrop's is extreme: 0.833, where a
normal text face is nearer 0.70. Its lowercase is nearly as tall as its capitals.

  Nunito's ratio is 0.694, the LOWEST of every candidate measured. So it had the closest
  x-height and the worst proportion at the same time: small lowercase under towering
  capitals, sitting next to a face built the opposite way. That mismatch is what reads as
  a stock pairing rather than a chosen one.

WHY FAMILJEN GROTESK WINS, IN ORDER OF HOW MUCH EACH IS WORTH

  - CLOSEST PROPORTION TO DARUMADROP OF ANY FACE THAT IS ALSO A READING FACE. 0.769
    against 0.833, less than half Nunito's distance. Baloo 2 ties on this number and loses
    on apparent size by seven per cent, which is the fault section 121.8 is already an open
    argument about.

  - APPARENT SIZE IS STILL PRESERVED, which was the first pass's real finding and is kept.
    Minus 2.0 per cent against Darumadrop, marginally better than Nunito's minus 2.2, so no
    layout number in this repository moves. Archivo would have grown every caption by 3.1
    per cent instead.

  - IT DRAWS EVERY GLYPH THE FRONT END ASKS FOR, AND IT IS THE ONLY CANDIDATE THAT DOES.
    Zero missing of the fifteen this repository has recorded as needed. Darumadrop cannot
    draw the two triangles or the check; Nunito could not draw those three OR the left
    arrow. Every one of those was falling through to a fallback face, which is a third
    typeface on screen that nobody chose. The specimen sheet shows it: every other
    candidate renders tofu boxes on that line.

  - IT IS NARROWER, WHICH IS FREE SAFETY. MenuKit.Label OVERFLOWS rather than wrapping
    (section 133.3's named trap), so a sub face that sets the same sentence shorter is
    margin that costs nothing. In the specimen the ability description fits inside its
    column in Familjen Grotesk and runs past it in all four of the others.

  - THE BOLD IS REAL, +51 per cent stem. That is the fault section 133 exists for and every
    candidate clears it; it stopped being the differentiator once the stem was measured on
    a plain cap I rather than on a tailed lowercase l. The first pass's table measured the
    l and reported weights that were partly its tail.

  - 58 KB A WEIGHT, the smallest of the set and less than half Nunito's. 116 KB of font in
    the Android player against 250.

  - AND THE SKELETON IS FIRM WHERE DARUMADROP IS SOFT, WHICH IS THE OPPOSITE OF THE FIRST
    PASS'S ARGUMENT AND IS WHY THAT ARGUMENT WAS WRONG. Nunito was picked partly for
    "sharing Darumadrop's family resemblance": rounded terminals, humanist warmth. Two warm
    rounded faces on one screen do not read as a pairing, they read as one face done twice,
    and the softer one loses. Familjen Grotesk is a grotesque with flat terminals and a
    slightly idiosyncratic a and g, so Darumadrop stays the only soft thing on the screen
    and keeps all of the personality. docs/TODO.md 133.7: "Darumadrop carries the quirk,
    the body face carries the reading."

WHAT IS DRAWN IN WHICH FACE

The split is not decorative. docs/TODO.md section 133.7, and it is what lets a screen be
quirky and calm at the same time. MenuKit.Face is the enum; MenuKit.Apply is the only place
that assigns either file; PaperKit.FaceFor takes the SIZE so a caller never chooses.

  DISPLAY (Darumadrop)         Screen headings, the Display, Title and Body type steps, the
                               ONE primary action per screen, player and hero names, big
                               values, buttons, settings row labels, the pennants.
  SUB     (Familjen Grotesk)   Caption and below: every sentence, hint, placeholder, field
                               label, ability description, chat line, empty state.

  A LABEL NEVER SETS FontStyle.Bold. MenuKit.Apply swaps the Bold FILE instead, because
  Unity's synthetic bold is the fault this whole pass exists to remove.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
ON THE INSTANCING, BECAUSE "UNMODIFIED" WOULD OTHERWISE BE A CLAIM THIS FILE CANNOT MAKE

Familjen Grotesk ships upstream as a single variable font. Unity's legacy Text takes a
static face, so the two weights here are instances cut at wght 400 and wght 700 with
fontTools, with updateFontNames on so the Bold file reports itself as Bold rather than as a
second Regular. No outline, metric or kern value is edited: an instance at a named axis
position is the same design the variable font draws at that position. The OFL permits this
and requires the licence to travel with it, which is OFL-FamiljenGrotesk.txt beside this
file, and the credits screen carries the attribution (CreditsContent).
