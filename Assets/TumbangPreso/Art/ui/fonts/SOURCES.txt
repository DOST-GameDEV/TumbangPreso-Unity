THE TWO FACES THIS GAME IS SET IN, AND WHAT THEIR LICENCE IS

This repository is public and the game is a competition entry, so every face here is under
the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and the licence text is committed beside it. Neither font is
modified: both are the upstream release, byte for byte.

docs/TODO.md section 133 is the work that added the second one, and it says why there are
two rather than one.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Darumadrop One, by Maniackers Design
   https://github.com/ManiackersDesign/darumadrop
   SIL Open Font License 1.1

   THE DISPLAY FACE. Headings, the Display and Title steps of PaperKit's type scale, the
   primary action on a screen, hero and player names. It is a rounded high-personality
   face and it is exactly right on a word somebody LOOKS AT.

   It ships ONE WEIGHT, which is the whole reason for the face below.

2. Nunito, by Vernon Adams, Cyreal and Jacques Le Bailly
   https://github.com/googlefonts/nunito
   SIL Open Font License 1.1, OFL-Nunito.txt in this folder
   Regular (usWeightClass 400) and Bold (700), from Google Fonts v32

   THE BODY FACE. Sentences, settings rows, chat, form fields, captions, hints: everything
   somebody READS rather than looks at.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY NUNITO AND NOT ONE OF THE OTHER THREE THAT WERE MEASURED

Four candidates were rendered at the sizes this game actually draws at, including the 4:3
1024x768 worst case where one canvas unit is 0.533 physical pixels. scratchpad/fontsrc/
holds the measurement script and the specimen sheets. The numbers that decided it:

                        x-height   x-height   stem 'I'   stem 'I'   file per
                        per-mille  vs Daruma  regular    bold       weight
  Darumadrop One          510         --         82        NONE      359 KB
  Nunito                  499       -2.2%        82        128       125 KB
  M PLUS Rounded 1c       520       +2.0%        86        154      3400 KB
  Figtree                 500       -2.0%        85        137       36 KB
  Baloo 2                 475       -6.9%        81        152      418 KB

  - APPARENT SIZE IS PRESERVED, which is the one measurement that mattered most. Every
    layout number in this repository was taken against Darumadrop's metrics, and what a
    label LOOKS like at a given fontSize is driven by x-height rather than by em size.
    Nunito's x-height is within 2.2 per cent of Darumadrop's, so moving a row to the body
    face does not silently shrink it. Baloo 2 would have cost seven per cent on every
    caption in the game, under a floor that is already being argued about in section 121.8.

  - THE BOLD IS REAL. Darumadrop has no bold face at all, so every FontStyle.Bold in this
    front end was legacy Text drawing each glyph twice at an offset: a smear rather than a
    weight, worst at the 18-unit floor where most of the game's words live. Nunito's Bold
    is a drawn weight, 56 per cent more stem than its Regular.

  - THE SKELETON MATCHES AND THE PERSONALITY DOES NOT. Nunito is a humanist sans with
    rounded terminals and open apertures. It shares Darumadrop's family resemblance, which
    is what stops the pairing reading as two games, and drops the wobble, which is what
    lets it carry a four-line ability description. M PLUS Rounded 1c is the same rounded
    lineage and was the runner-up on every count except the last two.

  - 125 KB AGAINST 3.4 MB. M PLUS Rounded 1c carries 8,201 glyphs because it covers kana
    and kanji, and this game is set in Latin. Two Nunito weights cost 250 KB in the
    Android player; two M PLUS weights would have cost 6.8 MB for glyphs nothing draws.

WHAT IS DRAWN IN WHICH FACE

The split is not decorative. docs/TODO.md section 133.7: "Darumadrop carries the quirk, the
body face carries the reading", and that split is what lets a screen be quirky and calm at
the same time. MenuKit.Face is the enum; MenuKit.Apply is the only place that assigns
either file.

  DISPLAY (Darumadrop)   Screen headings, the Display and Title type steps, the ONE primary
                         action per screen, player and hero names, big values, the pennants.
  BODY    (Nunito)       Every sentence, settings rows, captions, chat, form fields and
                         their hints, secondary and tertiary buttons, list rows, tooltips.

  A LABEL NEVER SETS FontStyle.Bold. MenuKit.Apply swaps the Bold FILE instead, because
  Unity's synthetic bold is the fault this whole pass exists to remove.
