Changes by the Tumbang Preso project: isolated one shoe where a source contained a pair,
normalised each prop to the game's 0.432 m gameplay length, seated and centred the mesh,
recoloured it to the game's role-safe palette, and added a narrow bevel for the in-engine toon
key light. No source trademarks or text are retained.
