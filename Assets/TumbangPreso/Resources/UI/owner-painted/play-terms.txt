TUMP is a game built around the Filipino street game tumbang preso. These terms explain the rules for using the game, creating an account and taking part in its online features. Read them before choosing I AGREE. If you do not agree, leave this page and do not complete account registration.
Use the game only when you are legally able to agree to these terms. If local law requires a parent or guardian to authorize your use, ask them to review the game and these terms with you. A device owner's permission is separate from permission to use another person's account. Nothing on this page removes rights that applicable law does not allow to be removed.

2. YOUR ACCOUNT AND SIGN-IN
Use an account that belongs to you or that you are authorized to use. Choose a password you do not share with other players, and do not impersonate the development team, moderators or another person. Keep enough information to recognize the account you created, especially when sharing a device.
Creating a username and password links the device's current progress to that account through the game's account system. Check which account is active before changing profile details or continuing on a shared computer. An optional contact email entered on the registration form stays on this device. It is not an email sign-in or an email password-recovery service. Do not rely on it to recover a lost password.

3. GUEST PLAY, LOCAL SAVES AND SHARED DEVICES
Guest progress and settings are stored locally. Local files may be lost if the game is removed, the device is reset, files are deleted or storage is damaged. A local save is not the same as a confirmed online account record. Online features may show the last information received when their services are unavailable.
Sign out or close the game when you finish using a shared device. Do not inspect, copy, alter or use somebody else's saved account information. Moving between guest and signed-in play does not give permission to overwrite another person's account. The game must be allowed to finish a save or synchronization before you assume a change has reached another device.

4. FAIR PLAY
Play through the controls, rules and features the game provides. Do not use unauthorized software, modified network messages, stolen credentials or deliberate exploits to gain unfair information, change results, interfere with other players or bypass account restrictions. If you discover a fault, report it rather than repeatedly using it against others.
Built-in bots, practice tools, custom-room rules, remapping and accessibility settings are legitimate game features. Use them within the mode or room that offers them. An organized tournament may publish additional event rules; read those rules separately. A good strategy, an unusual play or a strong opponent is not evidence of cheating by itself.

5. OTHER PLAYERS, NAMES AND CHAT
Treat people with respect in names, profile text, chat and shared content. Do not threaten, harass, discriminate against or impersonate others. Do not share another person's private information, passwords or account details. Avoid content that is unlawful, sexually exploitative, hateful or designed to deceive players into revealing information or visiting unsafe sites.
Use blocking, room controls and reporting features where they are available. Give accurate details when reporting a problem, and do not use false reports to punish someone for winning or disagreeing with you. A report is information for review, not proof that a player has broken a rule. Other players can see and may save messages you choose to send.

6. ROOMS, INVITATIONS AND CONNECTIONS
Share a room code only with people you want to invite. A private or unlisted room is not a place to exchange confidential information: people who receive its code may be able to join. Friends, party and room features depend on the services and permissions available in the current build.
Online matches need a working connection. Delays, disconnections, unavailable services or a host leaving can interrupt play. Reconnection features may preserve a seat or restore part of a match when the rules allow, but they do not guarantee that a match pauses or can always be recovered. Leaving a match can affect its record or access to queues according to the game's displayed rules.

7. RESULTS, RANKS AND COMPETITIVE RECORDS
Scores and match outcomes are determined by the game's rules and authoritative match state. A preview, animation or message on one device does not independently award points or establish a final result. Progress, rankings and match history may need time to synchronize after play.
Do not submit fabricated results, manipulate reports, arrange dishonest outcomes or attempt to claim rewards for matches that were not legitimately recorded. Fixes to errors or abuse may require records to be corrected. Rankings describe play within the game's current systems; they are not a promise of a prize, payment, selection for an event or permanent placement.

8. TANSAN, UNLOCKS AND TASKS
TANSAN is a game currency earned through the supported play and task systems. In this build it is not real money, cannot be withdrawn as cash and is not offered as a real-money purchase. Heroes, equipment and other unlocks give access within the game; they do not transfer ownership of the underlying artwork or software.
Online purchases and task claims are checked by the server. A local display or cached balance is not permission to grant an item or duplicate a reward. Check the item's name, ownership state and cost before confirming an action. Prices, task availability, eligibility and balance may change as the game is developed. Do not sell accounts, exchange TANSAN for cash or advertise unsupported trading services.
