ABILITY EFFECT SHEETS: WHERE THEY CAME FROM AND WHAT THEIR LICENCE IS

Every file in this folder is a DERIVATIVE. None of them is the artist's file as downloaded:
tools/build_vfx_sheets.py recolours each one into this game's hero families, because the
sources arrive in cobalt blue, cornflower and orange and the heroes here are amber, teal, red,
yellow and purple. The cell grid, the frame order and the drawing itself are untouched.

docs/Asset_Sourcing.md is the full source list and the rules that decide what may ship.
docs/TODO.md section 131 is the work.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PVFX Foundry, by Pixel VFX Studio
   https://nerijs.itch.io/pvfx-foundry
   CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
   https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

   Commercial use, modification and redistribution allowed. No credit required; the pack
   suggests one and it is given here anyway.

   Eleven of the twelve sheets here are recoloured PVFX Foundry effects, 96 x 96 at 20 FPS:

     vfx_rupture_v1     earth-rupture        vfx_bloom_v1       spectral-bloom
     vfx_frostnova_v1   frost-nova           vfx_bolthead_v1    magical-projectile
     vfx_emberjet_v1    ember-jet            vfx_smoke_v1       smoke-puff
     vfx_spark_v1       electric-impact      vfx_dust_v1        landing-dust
     vfx_implosion_v1   void-implosion       vfx_shrapnel_v1    solar-shrapnel
     vfx_burst_v1       warm-explosion

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Lightnings, by hdst
   https://opengameart.org/content/lightnings
   CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)

   vfx_bolt_v1 is one 512 x 512 texture cut into eight 64 px columns and graded onto Zack's
   amber ramp. Each column is one alternative stroke; a strike chooses one rather than
   playing them in order.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOT USED, AND RECORDED SO NOBODY HUNTS FOR IT: the CC0 Four Summoning Circles by Luke.RUSTLTD
(https://opengameart.org/content/4-summoning-circles) is the source docs/Asset_Sourcing.md
maps to Phaister's Hex, and it is deliberately absent. Her ward is conformed to the road by
VfxShapes.DrapeToGround because a circle that ignored the kerb was reported by name, and a
four vertex quad has nothing to conform with. tools/build_vfx_sheets.py carries the full
argument and the route back in.

NO ATTRIBUTION IS LEGALLY REQUIRED FOR EITHER OF THE TWO SOURCES ABOVE. It is recorded because
a licence that lives only in a commit message is a licence nobody can find, and because
Attention.md section 7.2 asks for every sourced asset's licence to be readable by a person
before it ships.

NOTHING FROM A UNITY ASSET STORE PACKAGE OR A SONNISS LIBRARY IS IN THIS FOLDER. Those may
ship inside a compiled player but their raw files may not enter a public repository, and this
repository is public. docs/Asset_Sourcing.md rule 8.
