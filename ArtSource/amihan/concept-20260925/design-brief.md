## 2. Research

**Vigan, Ilocos Sur.** A UNESCO heritage city: Calle Crisologo's cobbles and
Spanish-colonial stone-and-brick houses with capiz windows, kalesa carriages, burnay jars.
Its living craft is **Abel Iloko** (inabel), handwoven cotton; the Viva Vigan **Binatbatan**
Festival is named for beating cotton pods to free the fibre for the loom.

**The whirlwind in the weave.** The Ilocano **binakol** technique weaves straight lines into
optical curves; its patterns were said to confuse and turn away evil spirits. One of them is
**kasikus, "whirlwind"**: graduated rectangles radiating from a centre. A wind hero from a
weaving city wears the whirlwind her family weaves. It is the emblem on her capelet's back.

**The wind.** The **amihan** is the cool northeast wind of the dry season, strongest down
the Ilocos coast; in Philippine mythology Amihan is a long-haired woman who brings cool
breezes and protects fishermen. The Ilocano god of wind is Apo Angin. Amihan is also a real
Filipino given name, and it is hers. As with the cast, the wind is her talent, not a deity.

Sources: Wikipedia "Binakael", "Inabel", "Amihan (mythology)"; Yuchengco Museum "Weaving the
Story That Is the Binakul"; Tawid News on the Binatbatan Festival.

## 3. Who she is (the lore, also in docs/CHARACTER_ORIGINS.md)

Amihan grew up in the rooms behind her family's loom in Vigan, where the street outside was
a court whenever the kalesas stopped running. She learned to throw on cobblestones that
send a slipper anywhere but straight, and to read the wind off the abel hung out to air.
She is bright, proud of her town and hates a stalled game: when a match slows she is the one
moving, calling, dragging the court back into motion. Her weakness is the same thing:
she will commit to a play before it is ready rather than wait for it. Sean's patience
drives her up the wall. She teases Rafi that his current goes nowhere without her wind.
Cheska, the other northerner, is her favourite argument about whose mountains are colder.

## 4. The design, and the one open colour question

- **Silhouette:** the swept wavy hair and flower cluster at the head; the capelet across the
  shoulders; the long cream robe opening over shorts; the sash hanging at one hip.
- **Ink-only face** (method section 2): a bright, confident face, not the concept's ":3".
- **Cotton-boll hairpin:** the concept's white flowers read as cotton bolls with woven
  tassels, the Binatbatan's fibre. Hand-set.
- **Kasikus on the capelet's back:** graduated rectangles radiating from a centre, drawn by
  hand as decals (method section 4).
- **Colour:** the concept's teal sits in the same family as Rafi's sea teal. A hero owns a
  hue no other hero wears (method section 3), so her builder carries two palettes behind a
  switch, the concept's teal and an abel indigo, rendered beside the cast for the owner.
