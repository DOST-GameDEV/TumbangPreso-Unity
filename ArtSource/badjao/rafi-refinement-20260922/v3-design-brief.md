# Rafi distinctive hero revision, 2026-09-22

Owner rejected the copied-builder draft's face and hair, then clarified that the
whole character feels like a retexture. This revision changes silhouette and
garment construction while retaining the native skull/proportions/rig and only
editing Rafi's own builder. No gills; no existing hero changes.

Built-in image generation, no API/CLI/reset. The tool has no model-version selector.
Output: rafi-distinctive-hero-v3.png. References: actual native hero lineup in
validation Logs/rafi-native-voxel-v2/hero-lineup-front.png, plus the rejected
rafi-hero-concept-v2-no-gills.png as an identity starting point, not a finished design.

The generation brief asked for one front/quarter/back sheet plus face/FPP insets,
matching the cast's huge chunky head, short legs, simple no-thumb hands, flat
graphic face and cel colour. Direction: a hooked swept hair crest, exposed temple,
compact tied tail, narrow navy headwrap with an orange float clip; focused native
eyes/brows and a short near-horizontal closed mouth. An asymmetric sea-green
overshirt, cream shoulder lining, navy off-centre tie, tapered repaired sailcloth
hip panel and fitted rope coil should distinguish the actual forms. Warm brown
skin, deep brown hair and restrained navy/sand/sea-green colour, with tiny orange.
Explicit exclusions: another hero's hat/horns/bolt fringe, realistic long-legged
anatomy, armour/weapons, large gadgets, gills/fins/scales, invented ethnic markings,
tribal patterns and noisy faceted textures. The image is ideation, not Unity proof.

Implementation choices from the sheet:

- Native model proportions govern over the illustration's longer legs.
- Tilted chamfered hair pieces carry the sweep; retain the native rounded skull.
- One head float clip; omit a second duplicate hanging orange token.
- One left wrist cord. The generated hand inset duplicated it on both hands, but
  the actual body and first-person meshes must agree on the one chosen side.
- Two quiet sailcloth seams and one rope coil. These are individual practical
  belongings, not a claim about traditional clothing or sacred/community symbols.
- A shorter, lighter mouth/brows than the concept's large sharp marks keep his
  expression legible within the existing cast's graphic face construction.

Rejected prior face/hair source, model and palette are preserved under
ArtSource/rafi/rejected-face-hair-20260922. Current native review is versioned
separately and cannot be replaced by this concept sheet.
