in-engine review later. Do not copy its heavy outlines or detailed garment facets
automatically. There is no approval for fingers, thumbs or realistic anatomy.

is original concept detail, not an authenticated traditional pattern. No specific
cultural tattoo or sacred design has been established or adopted.

## Map alternatives

`map-compositions-v1.png` compares A/neighbourhood boardwalk,
B/community jetty and C/sheltered lagoon. These are composition studies: broad
court, fixed house supports, boats, usable access and community at the edges.

The generated wood/water/textile detail is more elaborate than the actual game
and is not a proposed texture pack. Later production should retain readable
chunky house mass and simplify material detail. Court dimensions, access widths,
supports, edge recovery and traversal have not been measured or accepted from
these illustrations. A pretty scene is not a playable map contract.
