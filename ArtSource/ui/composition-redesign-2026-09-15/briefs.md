# Generation briefs and selection record

All calls used the built-in image tool at the owner's explicit request. Exact
model version and deterministic recreation controls were not exposed. Artwork
is kept separate from native logo, text, controls, progress and runtime evidence.

## Home initial brief

16:9 background only. Quiet left38percent for menu, can/flip-flop lower right,
chalk line into a modest Filipino neighbourhood/store/tree/laundry. Deep red
brush contours and warm peach/coral/ochre/olive, broad shapes, low texture budget.
No people, text, logo, controls, floating props or orange patterned wallpaper.
First output d23638c7-780b-47e6-b9d3-37ec35ac1c7d was rejected because its realism,
rust/plaster/leaf detail overwhelmed the supplied hand-painted style.

## Home style correction

Edit the first image while retaining layout. Use the actual TUMP(5) sheet only
as a style reference; do not copy its logo/control pixels into the background.
Flatten detail to two/three tones per object, red/brown contours, grouped leaves,
simple store shapes and broad shadows. Keep the menu area quiet and no baked UI.
Output8ab3a147-35ce-452c-a59a-aa2ef8e292ee is home-court-v2, integrated for review.

## Loading brief

Independent16:9 low court-level diagonal composition. Airborne slipper travelling
toward a small grounded can in a chalk circle. Modest street/store/tree/laundry,
quiet dark lower-left for a native tip. Chunky painted poster style, wine contours,
ochre/peach, terracotta/olive and dusky sky. No people or generated text/controls,
no giant blank wall and no reused orange wallpaper. Output271a49c1-a8c2-4e8c-b2d7-
611ee7bb3f41 is loading-street-v1, integrated with real progress and optional stories.

Original outputs remain in the local generated_images task folder. These portable
copies and their hashes are authoritative if the original workstation changes.
The source rasters are1672x941. Native text/shapes remain resolution-independent;
all PC-size checks must distinguish layout support from source bitmap resolution.

## Account gate v1 and v2

court gate, small distant can, two slippers, native-logo room upper-left and quiet
dark lower quarter for captions. Warm Philippine neighbourhood, no people/text/UI.

V2 targeted edit preserves the gate composition and takes style only from original

wall/roof/gate face, remove realistic wear/flecks. Original owner image unaltered.
